# Threatened workplace: organizer review

This local, low-fidelity review artifact is **Part of PER-331**. It demonstrates
one political decision with material evidence available on demand. It implements
no Babylon action and does not complete PER-331 or PER-332 acceptance.

Open [index.html](index.html) in a browser. The example needs no server, installation, network
access, account, database or build. Tab and Enter operate controls.

Escape closes an evidence inspector and returns focus to its opener. The draft,
selected approach, selected workplace and period survive inspection in the same
tab. Refresh resets the example. It does not save a campaign or a durable draft.

## Authority and limits

The [canonical Linear design brief](https://linear.app/percy-raskova/document/organizer-first-ui-and-universal-two-line-struggle-design-direction-f7d8195319f2)
and [PER-331](https://linear.app/percy-raskova/issue/PER-331/design-the-organizer-first-political-decision-workspace)
own this direction. [PER-332](https://linear.app/percy-raskova/issue/PER-332/design-universal-two-line-struggle-contested-proposals-and)
owns the broader source, ontology and universal line-struggle design. This artifact follows both issues
and their comments. This README is a concrete handoff,
not a replacement design authority, roadmap, source study or new ADR.

The user's session decisions select the threatened-workplace case. An admitted special
commitment replaces standing practice for one period. The same routine resumes if
eligible and otherwise pauses for review. The player supplies the final organizational ruling.
No NPC vote can substitute another line after an eligible ruling. Independent
partners, normal admission and actual implementation capacity still constrain
what occurs.

All political actors, reports, positions, eligibility, organizational hours,
practice results and partner responses are **design fixtures**. The 16 available
organizer-hours, 8-hour routine/reinforcement and 12-hour inquiry are interface
examples, not calibrated values or conversions into engine CL. Unspent hours do
not grant a second practice. The geographical panel is a location placeholder,
not county geometry. The artifact uses the existing Cold Collapse CSS tokens.
Historical design files and their extraction manifest remain unchanged.

The material inspector identifies the proposed Wayne maintenance witness.
The consumer is `26163-31-33-metal_parts` and the provider is `owner-26163-81`.
Every case starts with 2,560 kg consumer metal stock and 16 enabled batches.
Provider spares are 256 kg versus zero. Its employed/reserve counts are 1/0 versus 0/1.
Each job uses 1 kg plus 10 current person-hours, with a ceiling of 16 jobs.

The common finite 256 kg restock order has a predicted first
60 kg grant after maintenance. The 16-versus-zero first-period jobs,
960-versus-zero second-period output and delayed 960 kg recovery are
**scenario fixtures/predictions, not qualified runtime results**. They show
the information relationship only. Production recovery does not prove wage
recovery, solidarity, political readiness or the effectiveness of an organizer
action. The completed screen deliberately does not credit the political choice
with material recovery.

## Connected review route

**Routine:** inspect an attributed quiet-period result. An explicit scope uses
one practice slot. A pinned income-protection intention executes nothing.

**Reduced shifts:** a workplace committee reports a material problem. The
committee is not equated with the entire workforce. The player's organization
controls its own response, not workplace scheduling or another body's consent.

**Inspection:** open material evidence, the existing relationship, and Direction
and disagreements. A finite inspector preserves the decision context and draft.
The physical cause is supporting evidence. A production matrix is not required
to understand the immediate concern.

**Approaches:** compare Inquiry, Reinforce the existing eligible workplace tie,
and Hold. Inquiry spends more capacity to pursue knowledge and risks delay.
Reinforcement supports a bounded existing relation without resolving every
uncertainty. Hold continues the neighborhood routine and its cost while leaving
the workplace problem unresolved. No option receives a success probability.

**Player ruling:** review our scope, allocation, displaced routine, preserved
objection, cooperation limits, resolving period and revision condition. Confirm
the selected direction. Safe refusal retains the draft and records no practice,
expenditure or grant. Admission is distinct from universal endorsement.

**Submitted → resolving:** the fixture fixes the accepted record. Submission credits no effects.
One 28-day period resolves without weekly steps or a second routine practice. Cancellation and amendment are unavailable here
because their runtime contracts are not implemented.

**Completed:** show separate material, practice, knowledge/relationship, partner
and routine accounts. The independent partner can offer private inquiry only,
decline, or fail to reply. An inquiry can complete without gaining a
partner report. Silence does not mean consent.

**Appraisal:** compare stated direction, adopted intention and enacted practice.
Preserve the objection and reopen the remaining income/participation question.
Neither success nor failure supplies political correctness or automatic learning.

The **Pause standing work** control changes future routine authorization and
spends no routine hours. Hold adds no special commitment and leaves that
authorization alone. Explicit Pause does not auto-resume.

A special commitment replaces the routine only for Period 3. At feedback, eligibility allows the routine to resume with the same scope and
ceiling for Period 4. Otherwise it pauses with a safe review message, without
inventing another target. The fixture
does not allow changes to an already submitted Period 3 allocation.

Expand **Reviewer controls** before submission to choose a partner response,
eligible/ineligible routine resumption, or admission refusal. These are authoring
controls outside the player workflow, not powers to choose another actor's
response. At appraisal, **Compare another ruling** reopens the same authored
starting conditions and preserves the draft. This does not roll back a campaign.

## Information placement and authorization

| Surface | Player question / information | Boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Default situation and compact bar | Whom do we represent, what is at stake, what have we committed, what remains available? | Show only modeled and permitted accounts. This example's hours are fixtures. |
| Relationship and disagreement inspector | What did someone report, who made the claim, what do others contest, and what cooperation do they offer? | Support, ruling, participation and capacity remain separate. No hidden membership counts appear. |
| Material context and history | What dependence can explain this interruption, at what period and in what units? | The authoring comparison here is admin/reference evidence, not permission for a future player to read raw observer truth. |
| Archive/reference | Which report or completed receipt supports a claim? When was it obtained and is it still current? | Filter before retrieval and rendering. Inspection depth cannot remove fog. |
| Admin/debug | Source hashes, hidden-state comparison, fixture response controls, raw circuit parameters | Never count these surfaces as evidence of player action. |

Report tags need separate meanings. **Known** is an authorized fact.
**Reported** is an attributed claim. **Inferred** names a conclusion and its
evidence. **Stale** keeps the last known period. **Unknown** has no permitted reading.

The `Unmodeled` label means no mechanic exists. **Unavailable** means the system
cannot currently supply a reading or action. **Zero** requires a completed
quantitative reading.

These are design requirements for lawful projection, not new permissions or a
universal confidence scalar. Own-organization reports do not reveal private minds.
Equal lawful knowledge must produce the same visible preview and safe refusal
across hidden-state twins. This static artifact cannot prove that runtime property.

## One-off verification

Review at 1366×768 and 1920×1080. Inspect the compact 720px layout and 200% browser
text scaling. The layout scrolls instead of covering the world with simultaneous
panels. Native button/textarea/dialog controls give keyboard focus. Selection
and lifecycle use text and borders as well as color. The example uses no animation, timer,
sound, dependency download or network access.

Exercise each approach, explicit Pause plus Hold, eligible and ineligible resume,
independent partner refusal/no reply, admission refusal, inspector Escape/return,
and draft retention. A one-off browser exercise and captures support the task
handoff outside production source. These check only the local interaction,
not native Bevy integration, a human comprehension test, replay, knowledge
noninterference, standing-work mechanics or maintenance predictions.

The local Chromium exercise covered all three approaches, Hold plus explicit
Pause, eligible/ineligible resumption, independent responses, safe refusal and
draft/selection/focus retention. It found no page errors, external requests or
horizontal overflow at 1920, 1366, 720 and 683 CSS-pixel widths. The 683px check
approximates layout space at 200% zoom on a 1366px display. Actual browser zoom
and native text scaling still need review. The agent inspected rendered review
and comparison captures. Independent static review caught and corrected a
relationship-inspector sentence that prematurely implied routine displacement.
