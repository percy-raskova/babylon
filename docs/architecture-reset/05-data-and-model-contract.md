# 05 — Data and model contract

**Status: PROPOSED, pending the targeted governance changes.** This chapter makes the Director's
entertainment-first, national-with-uneven-detail direction concrete. It does not authorize silently deleting current
causal state, changing live saves, or treating an old receipt as unnecessary merely because it is old. The reset
deliberately changes representation and retention while preserving meaningful material consequences and permanent
player history.

## The model the game will retain

Keep all 3,144 domestic county identities and the current bounded foreign/dependency counterparts. County is the
default national economic location. H3 supplies geography, local presentation and checked allocation where a
scenario actually uses it; do not create a complete national fine-cell economic estate to support a map zoom.
Geography remains fixed. Changing map zoom never changes engine results.

Keep the current ten economic functions as the initial national vocabulary. A background workplace is a counted
county/function/ownership actor, not a real establishment replica. Households remain counted cohorts. Preserve
independent earning and owner-exposure axes, collective residence, eligible versus inactive population,
employment/reserve, cash and owned stocks, obligations and available time. Household identity must not imply every
person in that cohort has identical wealth or political behavior; label aggregates as Designed where their
construction is designed. The existing [household keys, lines
3–15](../../rust/crates/babylon-persistence/src/national_household_allocation/model.rs) and [joint population
margins, lines 13–61](../../rust/crates/babylon-persistence/src/national_household_refinement.rs) are the initial
conservation contract.

Foreground detail follows actual player relationships: the controlled organization and household principal, its
workplace, independently actionable known partner workplaces, and unresolved commitments or shipments involving
them. These receive separately addressable identities and permanent causal receipts. Other actors use the default
cohorts. A camera movement or a reader query cannot create people, cash, capacity, knowledge or a new mechanical
actor.

For this reset, decide foreground partitioning at campaign initialization from captured scenario inputs. Do not
implement automatic dynamic geographic refinement as part of the storage cutover. A later action that needs a new
separate actor must use an explicitly authored and journaled split rule with conservation and independent authority;
until such a rule is approved, that target remains an aggregate. This avoids accidentally inventing an untested
adaptive simulation engine.

Merge rows only when their causal keys are equivalent: same owner/principal, location, good/unit, route or resource
dependency, due period, price/settlement terms, uncertainty/choice identity, and player commitment/disclosure
status. Coalescing in-flight goods with different arrival dates or liabilities is invalid. Pooling wealthy owners
with wage-dependent residents, or sharing one time budget across unrelated principals, is invalid. Conversely,
repeated recipe coefficients and literal names need one definition, not thousands of expanded copies. Preserve exact
balances and quantities in retained relationships; reduce the number of relationships the model asks to represent.

## Authoritative owners

The implementation uses four owners:

1. **Captured content:** source identities, fixed geography, recipes, type declarations, rules, scenario policy and
   deterministic opening definitions. Store once; share immutable Rust allocations.
2. **Current causal state:** active graph, holdings, obligations, freight, capacity, workforce, equipment, current
   offers/plans, organizer authority and compact knowledge/resource indexes. One complete current root after every
   commit.
3. **Permanent journal and disclosure ledger:** accepted inputs, resolved action batches, governed choice receipts,
   player results, acquired observations, delivered dossiers and compact period summaries. History remains available
   without carrying every past row in the next-period working state.
4. **Recent technical detail:** exhaustive national receipt and diagnostic event families for the latest thirteen
   completed periods. Older full detail can be regenerated by exact replay, not represented as if it had been
   retained.

Canonical identity belongs to typed semantics, not JSON field order or SQLite row order. Static definitions are
referenced through typed IDs; current quantities and the explicit causal indexes enter the world root. A saved
journal prefix and disclosure prefix enter the snapshot manifest. Presentation navigation and the bounded Archive
work queue live in the independently writable `service_state` record from chapter 06; they are persisted and
integrity-checked but excluded from mechanical identity. All changing game effects still come through the engine.

Each inventory gives the source path and declaration line at the investigated head. A table's disappearance means
its data moves to the named owner, not that the material relation disappears. Do not port all 66 SQL tables into 66
SQLite tables. Do not remove the old implementation before consumers and historical/restart contracts have their
replacement. This is a new-format campaign path; old evidence and saves are preserved without a compatibility layer.

The Rust kernel's 36 material families remain available as in-memory closing results. Cutting old technical
retention does not suppress their production when current accounting or player consequences require them. All full
families enter recent technical detail for thirteen periods. Permanent extraction happens before that detail can
expire. Every period keeps compact production/consumption/unmet-demand, market-price, labor, public-budget and
material/cash-conservation summaries keyed by their retained game scopes. A player's relevant shipment, wage, aid,
investment, contact or collection consequence keeps its exact causal receipt even when the equivalent nationwide
family is later evicted.

The extraction rule is deterministic: retain a full item in the permanent journal if it references a
player-controlled principal, an accepted player commitment, an earned observation/contact/product, or a world event
explicitly designated consequential by the current scenario's captured rule declaration. Everything else contributes
only to the declared summary families and recent technical detail. An implementer cannot choose significance using
prose heuristics or an AI judgment. Version and hash the extraction policy with content.

## Causal state that must not be lost

An unpaid wage is current state. So is a reserve attached to a pending purchase, freight due next month, equipment
with remaining service life, a standing agreement, an unconsumed contact product and a household time debit. Their
age does not decide retention. Current `HistoricalCostBook` is primarily current carrying amounts and equity, not a
complete journal of old transactions; retain these values where future accounting consumes them.

Household-time receipts and contribution uses describe the last completed period and constrain available organizer
time. Keep their required exact current fields. Current ordinary, aid and collection receipts are also
cross-referenced by organizer validation. The replacement must retain immutable journal IDs plus the compact facts
needed to validate active agreements, products, acquired observations and resource use. Historical data does not
become a live index solely because a validator currently loops over the full vector. Replace that validator with one
over the admitted current indexes and authenticated referenced entries. See [organizer state, lines
322–334](../../rust/crates/babylon-practice-contract/src/organizer.rs), [receipt/product checks, lines
196–260](../../rust/crates/babylon-practice-contract/src/organizer/contract.rs), and [time use, lines
103–193](../../rust/crates/babylon-tick/src/material_world/organizer_time.rs).

The consumed-product/command indexes remain durable. A product ID cannot become usable again because its display
receipt leaves the recent window. Resolved identities may leave the hot index only when the identity construction
and current product roster prove they cannot reappear; this reset defaults to retaining compact tombstones. Those
tombstones count toward save growth. Ordinary UI receipt lists remain paginated, but the journal remains permanent.

A material backlog currently equal to ordered minus shipped is derived rather than separately authoritative in the
new format. Material labor schedules derived from the admitted graph-owned staffing are likewise rebuilt with exact
principal and time joins. This requires changes to canonical schema and consumers; it is not legal to delete a
stored field and assume an unrelated helper reconstructs it. All other field-specific decisions are in the
inventory.

## Integration and acceptance

The implementation must trace each changed inventory row from source or accepted input through current state,
commit, scoped reader and restart. For static/current separation, compare equal inputs through the next transition
and verify exact outputs where mechanics did not change. A Designed model reduction gets new scenario identity and
its own causal acceptance examples rather than claiming old whole-world hash parity.

Required gameplay controls are: wage-dependent and owner-exposed households react differently to disrupted
production; a bottleneck propagates beyond its county; an unfulfilled order remains unresolved across a restart; one
household's shared time cannot be spent twice through two organizations; delayed aid preserves donor goods,
recipient delivery and authority; known versus hidden facts remain distinct; and a player can find the permanent
receipt for a decision older than thirteen periods.

A smaller representation must improve measured ordinary play. Proposed targets are a 16 GB host, at most
4,000,000,000 bytes combined running-game memory, ordinary committed turns at most 30 seconds, and a 10
MB-per-period storage ambition. Neither dropping coverage nor hiding checkpoint spikes can qualify these. [The
runtime comparison](06-target-runtime-and-sqlite.md) separates backend savings from data-contract savings;
[retention accounting](07-history-replay-and-archive.md) states exactly what remains in the save.
