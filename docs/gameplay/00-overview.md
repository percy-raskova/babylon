# Babylon Gameplay Vision — porch sessions

Living record of game-design decisions (distinct from architecture plans in
`docs/architecture-reset/` and specs in `docs/superpowers/specs/`).
Entries are dated; newest thinking wins on conflict, older entries stay for
context. Rule of the directory: this is the *game* — what the player does,
feels, reads, and takes away. Engine internals appear only as they surface.
