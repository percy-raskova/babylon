# Porch session 01 — 2026-10-25: what Babylon is as a game

## The player

A leftist of some stripe — Marxist, DSA electoralist, the curious, even the
hostile. The DSA electoralist who downloads it to prove the designer wrong
by winning through elections is a first-class player: the game must let
them try, because the game has no teleology. Not a Paradox loyalist, not
here for graphics or twitch action. Here because an indie crank built a toy
no one else sells: vicarious revolution from the couch.

Nobody plays it *as* anything else. Not a Paradox game despite the
influence. Not an RTS. The fantasy is specific: run the experiments that
would be unwise or impossible in real life — the commune, the electoral
path, the Blanquist cell — inside a machine that feels real without
claiming to predict.

## The hook, in one line

Live the organizer fantasy, and feel Capital work. In the designer's
words: the player walks away having *felt* Capital work the way a novel
teaches — "Babylon taught me Capital, but it was fun."

## Honesty as design

- The bias is in the starting conditions, never in the endings. The game
  is openly ideologically biased and honest about it.
- Systems do what they do. No prescribed outcomes, no hidden rails, no
  designer-preferred victory. The game shows; it never prescribes.
- It should feel *real* — built on real maps, real systems, real economic
  care — without ever claiming to be a crystal ball or an oracle.
  Entertainment and fiction first; the data discipline serves the feel.

## Texture

Eerie. Disco Elysium's narrative company meets Victoria 3's systemic
weight, with the LLM as storyteller rather than garnish. Nested tooltips,
an in-game wiki, stories wild enough to screenshot and share. Underneath:
spreadsheets with a custom soundtrack — intricacy with a distinct flavor.

## The five screens

The player lives across five surfaces and picks whichever the moment
needs. Within a screen, information comes *to* them — assembled context,
exactly the statistics the tooltips owe — never a corporate dashboard to
troll through:

1. **Topology** — the graph of people, organizations, relationships, under
   fog. The unusual one; the graph-theory surface.
2. **Electoral** — elections, government policy, the state as contest.
3. **Physical map** — the United States, population, demographics, real
   geography (H3-tethered).
4. **Ticker** — the market breathing; the economy as a pulse.
5. **Wiki** — history, lore, mechanics, exportable. The permanent record.

Contemplative, weighty, seamless — closer to reading an intelligence
briefing than operating software. Thinking is slow; choosing is sharp
(see decision ceremony below).

## The decision moment is ceremony

Deliberation lives in the five screens (poring, weighing, wiki-diving).
The decision itself is a moment of drama: a split-pane event popup
(HOIV4-event energy, nicer face) laying the options side by side with
honest summaries of what each *means* — never what each *yields*. The
click is the gavel, not the deliberation. Physically: click, sometimes a
slider or typed number when allocating people or money. Sensory weight:
music, visuals, the feel of committing.

## The answer comes in three layers

The click is a command; the world answers on its own schedule.

1. **The seal (immediate).** Music sting, popup closes, receipt stub:
   "the split is declared." Known prices debited visibly on the spot.
   Unknowns marked unknown, honestly — "the party will react; you'll
   read about it." Outcomes are never previewed. The game states what is
   staked, which mechanisms engage, and what it does not know.
2. **The reckoning (period close).** The engine adjudicates in periods and
   publishes after the tick succeeds, so the answer arrives as *story
   first, numbers second*: ticker headlines, topology rewiring, a wiki
   entry in its own voice. Numbers underneath for audit; narrative first
   for humans. Automatically the shareable artifact.
3. **The long tail (periods later).** Some consequences arrive late by
   design — a betrayal surfacing three periods on through the knowledge
   system. The wiki backfills; the player *discovers*. Backdated
   knowledge (`observed_period` vs `acquired_period`) used dramatically,
   not just correctly.

**Hard rule:** the game never explains *why* mechanistically. It shows
what happened, who did what, what it cost. Causes are for the player to
infer, argue about, and post.

## The visibility rule (canonical)

Show only what a participant could learn from reading a communist
history book — Stalin's *History of the CPSU(B)*, Lenin's *What Is To
Be Done?*, above all Mao: witty, entertaining, accessible, never
skimping, never condescending. The player writes the history of their
world as if it were a political treatise; the history is the frontend
to the state machine.

Working test: *would Mao have put it in the treatise?* Funds seized —
yes, a fact in the world. Cohesion modifier — no, that's the author's
desk drawer. Concrete countable world-events (funds, chapters, votes,
crowds) may show; engine-computed abstractions (scores, multipliers,
trigger weights) stay buried. Statistics serve the fiction or stay
inside the machine.

**Failure mode:** spreadsheet popups. Visible math. Gameable multipliers.
The moment the player picks an option *because it's a 2.25x*, the
eerie-real thing dies. If they can see the formula, they'll play the
formula.

## The narrator: three authors, one record

From the Gramscian Wire vision (`ai/epochs/epoch3/gramscian-wire-vision.yaml`):
every significant event renders three ways from the same immutable
journal — the facts are pinned, only the meaning is contested:

- **Corporate Feed** — hegemonic euphemism, the official story.
- **Liberated Signal** — the movement's samizdat telling, witty and
  partisan. The Mao-voice book the player writes.
- **Intel Stream** — raw engine data, no adjectives, deterministic
  fallback.

Hegemony is a resource: when high, the Corporate Feed reads as
mainstream and the Signal as fringe; when it cracks, both sit side by
side. Rigorous, communist, not unbiased, not hagiography — hagiography
requires falsification, and the journal makes falsification impossible.
A history that must print its own defeats because the record says they
happened.

The player reads all three feeds and writes one.

## The LLM contract (binding)

The LLM writes the plot — Liberated Signal, wiki entries, chronicle. It
**describes and never modifies**. Its only write permission is a
markdown file. Its read permissions are god-mode. Narration is a pure
function of state; only the engine judges mechanics.

Prior art in-repo: the viable-game-design spec already constrains the
model to re-prose without adding claims (entailment eval), with
templates as the no-model fallback so nothing is ever empty.

## Fog and the leak problem (solved by existing design)

The omniscient narrator must never leak what the player must discover.
The fence is the same serialization boundary the screens already use
(`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-07-17-viable-game-design.md` §5):

- **Two-layer read model.** Material layer (production, wages, rent,
  demographics, territory) is public record. Political layer
  (consciousness, agitation, heat, stances) is visible only within
  organizing reach. Unknowns render as honest unknown — never zero,
  never stale. Clicking an unknown names what you don't know and how to
  learn it (INVESTIGATE composer).
- **Intel is a ledger**, event-sourced from INVESTIGATE resolutions,
  time-stamped, visibly aging. Visibility is a pure function of
  (graph, intel ledger) — the engine is untouched, determinism
  preserved.
- **The Veil of Money.** Conceptual visibility scales with theoretical
  development: Tier 0 sees prices; Tier 1 sees exploitation; Tier 2 sees
  the scissors. Locked instruments are visible-but-veiled *with a path*
  ("Study: Value Theory"), never hidden — which doubles as the
  onboarding engine for the unusual interface.
- The narrator reads through this exact boundary: frames are
  knowledge-sequenced to what the reader's side has acquired. God-mode
  reads go in; fenced prose comes out. The `acquired_period`
  machinery is the fence.
