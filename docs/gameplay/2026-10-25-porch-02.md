## 1. Epistemics: *On Practice*, indirect vs. direct experience, and fog that lies

### The two-stroke veil (Mao's *On Practice* + *On Contradiction*)

Political maturity is neither a tech-tree mana bar nor automatic XP from grinding.
It follows Mao's distinction in *On Practice* between **indirect experience**
(what past revolutionaries paid in blood to abstract into theory) and **direct
experience** (contact with the concrete particularity of *this* world):

- **Study (indirect experience / universal laws):** Your cadres do not have to
  invent Marxism in a cave or walk a five-person book club into a military base
  to learn that adventurism fails. Studying accumulated historical theory lifts
  the **conceptual veils**—unlocking the Veil of Money on the Ticker/Circuit and
  tearing away the liberal fiction on Topology ("the police only enforce the law;
  stay legal and you're safe") so your organization knows *what categories exist*
  (State attention threads $\Theta$, Lavender/Gospel risk scoring, labor
  aristocracy bribery). Because Babylon is a game, a player who skips reading
  and tries the five-man raid anyway gets taught the same lesson safely from
  their couch.
- **Practice & Social Investigation (direct experience / particularity of the
  graph):** No book from 1902 or 1937 can tell you *which* node in your Wayne
  County network is an informant, how stretched the governor's thread budget is
  in period 14, or whether a defeat happened because your line was wrong or
  because the balance of forces wasn't there yet (*Where Do Correct Ideas Come
  From?*). Only touching the graph—organizing, taking losses, running social
  investigations, and summing up your own campaign journal—populates the
  concrete map.

**Rule:** Study unlocks the *instrument* (what kind of relation you are looking
for); Practice and Investigation populate the *readout* (what is actually
happening in front of you).

### Fog that lies (`fog-of-war.yaml` + `viable-game-design.md` reconciled)

- **Zero contact = Honest Unknown:** Where your organization has neither
  presence nor reports, the surface renders an honest unknown (never zero).
  Clicking it opens a card naming what you don't know and how to learn it
  (`INVESTIGATE` / `SOCIAL_INVESTIGATION`).
- **Contact in hostile "Desert" territory ($M_r < 0.2$) or via a compromised
  node = The Dossier Lies:** Intelligence is relationship ("the fish in water"),
  not a scout unit. When you send a cadre into a hostile labor-aristocracy
  suburb (*The Suburban Ambush* / "Maple Heights") or read through an infiltrated
  node, **the dossier lies with a straight face**: it prints normal-looking
  solid text (`Calm / No Militia Detected`) while behind the mowed lawns the
  WhatsApp group and church-basement militia are preparing an ambush. You only
  earn **Water** (accurate reads, informant exposure, early warning of police
  raids) by building mass receptivity and conducting real social investigation.

## 2. Scale and dialectical physics: "the finest detail that's still an aggregate"

### Collective nodes, personification chrome

Babylon does not care about individual people as simulation units—it is never a
*Crusader Kings* soap opera and never simulates 340 million citizens.

- **Every node on the Topology graph is a collective entity**—a few thousand
  across the country that consumer hardware runs effortlessly: UAW Local 600,
  the Dearborn assembly plant, a Baptist church network, a county sheriff's
  department, a landlord association, a prison, a party chapter.
- **Individuals are personifications:** Named faces, portraits, and quotes are
  flavor and chrome sitting on top of a collective node to personify an economic
  or political category (just as Marx states in the Preface to *Capital* Vol. I).
  The figurehead is not the node (`research/04`, Leningrad 1925: a delegation or
  chair can be captured while 97% of the rank-and-file underneath can still be
  flipped by sending emissaries straight to the base).

### Contradiction is the engine of history and the UI

Normal strategy UIs present static bourgeois nouns with progress bars
("Stability: 74%"). In Babylon, **everything is a contradiction, framed as a
contradiction, moved by contradictions straining to overcome their barriers**:

- **Between nodes (edges):** Every pulsating line on Topology is an active
  tension between opposing poles (plant vs. union local, precinct vs. branch,
  prison town vs. captive urban semi-colony).
- **Inside every node:** Because every node is an aggregate, "one divides into
  two" operates inside every node on screen (reformist union brass vs. militant
  shop floor; party centralists vs. local adventurists; ideological "investors"
  vs. opportunistic "consumers").
- **Resolving a contradiction never produces "peace"—it births the next
  contradiction:** Winning a strike immediately creates the contradiction of
  contract bureaucracy vs. shop-floor power, or visibility vs. State heat.

### Three resolutions at the barrier ( shift / split / flip )

When an internal contradiction inside a collective node reaches its barrier
(modeled after Marx's *Mathematical Manuscripts* where finite differences cancel
at `0/0` into a new relation `dy/dx` with reversed roles, `research/09`), it
resolves in one of three ways:

1. **Non-antagonistic quantitative accumulation $\rightarrow$ Qualitative Shift:**
   The node transforms in place—its line leaps to a higher stage, its figurehead
   changes, and the collective stays whole.
2. **Antagonistic contradiction in a divisible collective $\rightarrow$ Split
   ("one divides into two"):** When two lines inside a movement, union, or party
   become irreconcilable, the node literally tears in two on the Topology
   canvas—spawning a breakaway node, partitioning membership and ties, and
   drawing a hot antagonistic edge between the two halves.
3. **Winner-takes-all contradiction in an indivisible seat $\rightarrow$ Flip:**
   Where the institution cannot divide (an official bargaining certificate, an
   executive board, a city council seat, a precinct), crossing the barrier
   forces an overthrow: one side takes the whole node, the other is ousted.

## 3. Topology: why it's fun, both sides' blind spots, and the two core moves

### The toy itself

Most players—even well-read Marxists—have never seen social power rendered as
topology. Turning what is under the hood a structured graph into a sleek, dark,
pulsating, colorful constellation gives the player the tactile thrill of sitting
at a revolutionary Palantir terminal: panning across living lines of force,
watching clusters heat up in crimson or go dark at period close, and clicking
node after node to open dossiers.

From Debord's *A Game of War* (`research/09`):
- **Lines of communication are the lifeblood:** A node's strength depends on
  whether its line of communication to an organizational centre/arsenal (press,
  legal fund, cadre school) is intact; sever the line and the node is paralyzed
  immediately.
- **Quantize the output, never publish the inputs:** Contests resolve to named
  qualitative states (**holds / dislodged / broken**), never hit-point bars or
  damage numbers.

### Both views on Topology are coarsenings with opposite blind spots (`research/08`, `17`)

- **The State is a Caesarist / Bonapartist ecosystem of competing Cellular Automata (`research/17`, `18`, `19`):**
  There is no single omniscient "State AI" brain. Every non-player agency,
  faction, and corporate bloc (City PD, County Sheriff, State Police, Fusion
  Center, FBI Field Office, **NSA**, **CIA**, **US Department of War**,
  Prosecutor/Court, Mayor/Council, National Guard, Prison Admin, Corporation,
  Chamber/Media) is a **cellular automaton** running `Capacity::allocate`
  (`babylon-graph`) over its own private dossier $L_c$, its own observation
  ceiling, and its own local objective function inside nested fiscal pools:
  - *City PD* optimizes call volume and clearance rate; *County Sheriff*
    optimizes occupied jail beds (per-diem revenue); *Fusion Center* has no
    arrest power so it inflates threat products to renew federal grants;
    *FBI* hoards files as title deeds to jurisdiction and protects its
    high-centrality informants (Malinovsky/Azef—even arresting loyal cadres
    above a mole to engineer the mole's promotion, per Serge's Okhrana
    directive!) from local police sweeps; *Mayor/Council* optimizes
    **preventing any agency from becoming autonomous**, underfunding
    coordination on purpose (RAND / Komer's *"everybody and nobody was
    responsible"*).
  - **The NSA ("The Eyes and Ears in the Ether"):** Pure signals/metadata
    panopticon with **zero boots on the ground and zero arrest power**. It sees
    100% of digital edges, wire transfers, and phone trees nationwide, and
    **0.0% of analog human contact** (shop-floor talk, prison kites, couriers).
    It can only act by passing stripped "parallel construction" referrals to
    the FBI/JSOC/PD, and can be blinded by going analog or fooled by spoofed
    digital decoy hubs.
  - **The CIA ("The Shady Guy Who Does Unspeakable Things"):** Legally barred
    from domestic policing, which means zero courtroom rules and 100% plausible
    deniability once the empire cracks (*Operation CHAOS, Phoenix, Gladio,
    Contra-crack* brought home), locked in a permanent turf war with the FBI:
    (1) *Strategy of Tension / Gladio false-flags* (funding ultra-left or
    fascist splinters to commit atrocities and pin them on your movement),
    (2) *The Contra-Crack Playbook* (flooding organized semi-colony/prison
    united fronts with narcotics and guns to shatter the *Peace* principle and
    generate off-books black revenue), (3) *Congress for Cultural Freedom*
    astroturf grants to split intellectuals, and (4) *Phoenix deniable
    "accidents"* against singleton leaders—which detonate a **Church Committee**
    crisis if your `INVESTIGATE` trail exposes the Langley cutout.
  - **The US Department of War ("The Behemoth Comes Home" — Fractal Boss Mode,
    `research/18`, `19`):** In normal play it faces outward (garrisoning the 12
    Trade Blocs and Israel to maintain $\Phi$). When QRF failure in multiple
    U.S. regions triggers Civil War, the Behemoth turns inward—immediately
    **weakening overseas garrisons and accelerating $\Phi$ collapse**, and
    unfolding into a **meta-cell of competing sub-cells**:
    - *Three Manpower Tiers:* **National Guard** (neighbors in uniform; pulling
      them up drains county QCEW workers, federalizing them under Title 10
      sparks a Governor-vs-Pentagon turf war, and near-zero social distance
      gives them the highest leak/mutiny rate), **Reserves** (post-Vietnam
      *Abrams Doctrine* logistics/medical/trucking spine—the soft belly of the
      war machine), and **Active Duty** (isolated fort-town imperial legions
      with `L ≈ 0` who treat Chicago or LA like Fallujah or Gaza).
    - *Service & Contractor Sub-Cells:* **Air/Drone Lavender & Gospel Command**
      (flattening whole city blocks in Chicago/LA by algorithmic proxy),
      **Heavy Ground Maneuver** (starving for FAF5 fuel/ammo tonnage that
      blockades can sever), **JSOC**, and **Defense-Industrial Primes**
      (optimizing munition burn and cost-plus replacement orders).
    - *Why its hulking strength is its weakness:* Leveling blocks in Chicago or
      LA **destroys Constant Capital ($K$) and real-estate collateral**, setting
      off a ferocious clash between **Finance-Capital** (Wall Street) and the
      **Department of War** (the Pentagon), while algorithmic strikes trigger
      100$\times$ boundary-of-the-cut backfire!
  - **Bonapartism as a fiscal derivative:** In crisis, tax/rent pools shrink
    and service-keyed budgets get cut while threat-keyed budgets grow—so
    `autonomy(c)` rises mechanically (*"the Caesar is the agency the
    depression did not defund"*).
  - **February 1917 as a type constraint (`candidates ⊆ L`):** An agency can
    only price targets resolved in its dossier $L_c$. The Okhrana drew two-foot
    colored ego networks (*Savinkov*) and 76-name liquidation charts (*Riga*),
    and lost to a bread queue because an unindexed spontaneous mass has no file
    in $L_c$—leaving only the blunt territory-level garrison (*Guard*), which
    is itself a cell with its own material grievance and morale.
- **The Player sees solidarity and misses opportunism (Weinstein + *Our Tasks*):**
  When your organization is poor and hunted, only committed *investors* join. As
  soon as a node wins clout, grants, or revenue, opportunistic *consumers* flood
  in—so your own success quietly rots a node's internal composition. Worse, per
  Stalin's *Our Tasks* (`research/04`), **a real wrecker must produce real
  successes** to keep cover: an infiltrated node never shows a lazy `-20%`
  modifier; it looks like a star performer right up until a load-bearing moment.
- **Note on the Newspaper (*Liberated Signal*) & Language Interface:** The
  newspaper is **a metaphor for the interface itself** (reading the three feeds
  and writing the movement's history and exposures), not a physical paper-truck
  supply-chain mini-game. And the "future language interface" mentioned in
  `NORTH_STAR.md` is parked as a potential post-v1 feature outside initial
  release scope.

### The two hands-on moves on Topology

1. **Internal Clarification (Splits, Purges & Rectification — `research/04`, `05`):**
   - *The split is how the party grows ("cohesion without clarification is rot
     with good manners").* Refusing to split ("Freedom of Criticism" / big-tent
     unity) gives a seductive honeymoon: fast membership growth, more dues,
     *falling* State heat, and **the Corporate Feed quoting you approvingly**—while
     your political exposures go soft and your giant org becomes a helpless
     liberal appendage.
   - Internal contradictions escalate through a four-stage state machine:
     `Fellow-traveller` $\rightarrow$ `Conciliator / Buffer` (worse than the
     deviation because it shields it) $\rightarrow$ `Faction` (secret press,
     separate dues) $\rightarrow$ `Antagonist`. The exact crossing into
     antagonism is **the moment the minority stops trying to win the
     organization and starts appealing to forces outside it**.
   - **The main danger is whichever deviation you have stopped fighting**
     (*Short Course* Ch. 11 §3: weight is a function of attention withheld, not
     intrinsic size).
2. **External Division Tactics ("Surrounded by oppressed and exploited we must
   unite. Surrounded by enemies, we must divide" — `research/06`):**
   In a net-exploiting core where the labor aristocracy ($W_c > V_c$) is bribed
   by imperial rent, your primary offensive verb on Topology is finding existing
   material contradictions *between* enemy or labor-aristocrat nodes and prying
   them open so "the proletariat passes through the cracks":
   - *Prison guards union vs. County Sheriff* over unpaid convict labor
     undercutting guard overtime.
   - *City PD vs. University PD* over jurisdiction and budget.
   - *Pension actuaries vs. Nativists/Minutemen* (the labor aristocracy's Social
     Security/Medicaid solvency materially requires the young migrant workforce
     it politically agitates to deport).
   - *Arms-plant / longshore strikes* (backed not for "bigger piece of the pie"
     wage demands, but because they choke the imperial war machine's supply line).

## 4. The Six Screens (Line / Doctrine approved as Screen #6)

1. **Topology** — The collective-node graph, active contradictions, State
   attention threads ($\Theta$), the Synopticon mirror, division tactics, splits
   and flips.
2. **Electoral** — Projected directly from Topology nodes (no free-floating
   "public opinion" bar). Built on the structural lessons of Victoria 3's script
   and binary engine (`research/10`, `11`) inverted through MIM(P):
   - Two currencies (`Clout` from wealth/institutional weight + armed force vs.
     `Votes` from the franchise), where decaying `electoral_confidence` halves
     vote weight and boosts wealth's political strength by $+50\%$, and where
     **votes go to the party while Clout divides the seats inside the coalition**
     (`CParty::GetVotingPowerFor`—so a union local in a popular front wins the
     election for its wealthier liberal partner).
   - Six real office verbs (**Space** [permits, halls, bulletin boards—MIM's
     carve-out], **Budget**, **Non-Cooperation** [sanctuary/carceral freeze],
     **Bail/Pardon/Charging** [DA/Sheriff races], **Tribune** [subpoenas that
     force hidden facts into the Intel ledger], **Appointments**).
   - **Refusal at incidence, never at the door:** The DSA electoralist can win
     real offices and pass real bills, then organically collide with the five
     material barriers: (i) capital strike & municipal bond downgrades
     (`unfunded` budget receipts), (ii) court injunctions & state preemption,
     (iii) police union insubordination, (iv) absorption / committee-chair
     staffer drift, and (v) the $\Phi$ ceiling (you won the election, and
     $W_c - V_c$ didn't move).
3. **Physical Map** — H3/county geography, demographics, contiguous internal
   semi-colonies (Black Belt/New Afrika, Aztlán, First Nations, Puerto Rico per
   Haywood and MIM(P)), two-sided prison siting (extracting urban captive nations
   to fund a rural white jobs program while recruiting a neo-colonial guard
   buffer), and visible county shifts from productive plant $\rightarrow$
   warehouse $\rightarrow$ call center (`csdraft.pdf`).
4. **Ticker** — The economy's pulse: wages vs. prices, cheap-import shocks,
   super-wage erosion, the Veil of Money instruments (T0 prices $\rightarrow$
   T1 exploitation $\rightarrow$ T2 the scissors), and **household time
   exhaustion** rendered as a concrete 168-hour weekly schedule
   $E = (T_{\text{wage}} + T_{\text{repro}} + T_{\text{commute}}) / 168$
   (Fortunati's double debt: a cadre misses meetings because the second shift
   ate her week, solved only by building childcare/mutual-aid institutions on
   Topology).
5. **Wiki** — The permanent, exportable record: Chronicle chapters titled by
   the period's dominant task in verb form (*Short Course*), `BRIEF SUMMARY`
   reckoning pages constrained by strict entailment, **vigilance case files** on
   deviations (symptoms, class content, how they were beaten), leaked enemy SOPs
   (Lavender/Gospel/IMF memos with human-cost footnotes), and cited empirical
   artifacts.
6. **Line / Doctrine (Approved 6th Screen)** — A branching map of ideological
   struggle (never a generic bonus tech tree): mutually exclusive trunks,
   MIM Theory 2&3's **three-strand line (class, nation, gender—no reduction)**,
   live degeneration traps (Imperialist Economism / "30 for 40" vs. "30 for 300",
   the PatSoc pipeline, Purity sectarianism, Bourgeois *Ms.*-style feminism,
   Faux-Vanguard LARP, the False Summit `RED_OGV`), and costly **Party Congress**
   ceremonies to ratify, split, or rectify.

## 5. The Three Resources & The Transmission Belt (`vanguard-economy.yaml`)

Each 28-day period, the player manages three non-fungible resources (with all
unconstitutional sigmoids stripped out in favor of state-snapshot ratios):

1. **Cadre Labor (CL):** Targeted, disciplined, specialized, predictable,
   consistent, and scarce. Trained slowly through political education or vetted
   promotion. The *only* resource that can build cells and independent
   institutions, run social investigations, execute surgical operations, or
   conduct rectification—and the **transmission belt** that steers mass energy.
2. **Money:** Hard funds from dues, press sales, or expropriations. Pays for
   printing presses, bail, legal defense, venues, and campaigns—with the material
   catch that easy money attracts opportunistic "consumers" (Weinstein).
3. **Supporter Labor (SL):** High-volume, volatile, vacillating, spontaneous
   mass energy that surges during crises and decays when idle. **You cannot
   target it surgically**—only channel it broadly into strikes, demonstrations,
   or ballot drives.
   - *Populist / Influencer Trap (huge SL, tiny CL):* "A million followers and
     zero soldiers"—your general strike decomposes into undirected riots and
     looting that tank consciousness and invite State repression.
   - *Sectarian / Reading Group Trap (all CL, no SL):* 100% discipline, zero
     mass weight in the world, picked off one by one.

## 6. The Vanguard Party Threshold & The 20-Year (260-Period) Clock (`research/12`)

### No Francis Fukuyama "End of History"

History never hits an arcade "GAME OVER" wall—the simulation continues and you
can always keep playing inside the world you made.

### The Two-Key Lock to Become a True Party

An organization starts as circles, cells, and fronts. Crossing into a **true
Party** (which starts the 20-year clock) requires a **two-key lock** where only
Key A belongs to the player:

- **Key A — The Act of Constitution (`constitute_party()` Congress):** The
  player calls a Founding Congress, adopts a written programme and rules,
  enforces **1903 Rule 1** (membership as personal participation in an
  organization, not Martov's "regular personal assistance"), establishes a centre
  with the power to **dissolve** subordinate bodies (*One Step Forward*), expels
  the Liquidators (Prague 1912), and runs an all-country newspaper supply chain.
  - *Call it early without the apparatus* and the engine gives you **Minsk 1898
    (The Paper Party)**: a grand founding headline on the Ticker while your
    committee is arrested the next period and Topology shows disconnected nodes.
- **Key B — Recognition by the Other Contingents (*WITBD* Ch. III):** *"It is
  not enough to call ourselves the 'vanguard'... we must act in such a way that
  all the other contingents recognise and are obliged to admit that we are
  marching in the vanguard."* Never a self-reported "Vanguard %" bar; the player
  observes Key B when **(1) rivals start arguing in your vocabulary, (2) the
  State reallocates its repression budget toward you (Zubatov's memo), and (3)
  adjacent unity fronts (the August Bloc) fracture or fuse into you.**

### The 20-Year (260-Period) SPD Calcification Clock (`survival_interest`)

Once your organization becomes a true Party, a **20-year (260-period) historical
window** opens—matching the generational arc of the Erfurt SPD (1891 $\rightarrow$
1914 = 23 years), the Second International (25 years), and the Bolsheviks
(1903 $\rightarrow$ 1917 = 14 years):

- Every legal institution you build (unions, newspapers, co-ops, elected seats)
  produces both capacity and an accumulating **`survival_interest`**—a
  constituency of staffers, editors, and officials whose livelihoods depend on
  preserving the legal shell under police law (*Collapse of the Second
  International* Ch. VIII: *"sold for a mess of pottage—organisations permitted
  by the present police law"*).
- **The Rule:** If 20 years (260 periods) pass after becoming a Party and you
  have not launched a revolution or civil war (or purged the careerist shell to
  spend the party as an instrument of power, Stalin's Feature 4), the shell's
  `survival_interest` overtakes the party. The Chronicle records that you
  **Failed**—calcified into a permanent Kautskyite SPD / revisionist CPUSA
  fixture of the bourgeois order—while letting you keep playing inside that
  accommodation.

## 7. Period-0 Seeded Start & The Law of the Boat

- **Seeded world perturbation bounded by `marxist-data-3NF.sqlite`:** A campaign
  seed deterministically perturbs initial conditions within the actual empirical
  variance of the 3NF reference database (never absurd values like `Pop = 0` or
  `GDP = 5 googolplex`; always within measured historical dispersion).
- **Player chooses starting geography and initial orientation at the door:**
  A player booting up Babylon already knows whether they want to test DSA
  electoralism, Maoist mass line, MIM(P) prison-ministry cells, syndicalism, or
  Blanquism, so they pick their starting city/region and initial tendency out of
  the gate.
- **The Law of Steering a Boat:** At Period 0, your organization is a dinghy
  starting from null—changing direction is as easy as it will ever be. As your
  movement grows into a heavy freighter with thousands of members, institutions,
  and `survival_interest`, its angular momentum grows with it: turning the wheel
  late in the game is slow, costly, and splits half the hull.

## 8. Dynamism, Differential Equations, Chaos Theory & The 33/33/33/0 Wealth Law (`research/13`, `14`)

### No magic constants—state-snapshot differential equations

Babylon rejects Paradox-style magic numbers (like V3's hardcoded
`country_officers_pol_str_mult = 3.0` or arbitrary `steepness_k` sigmoids):

- Every leverage weight and rate of change $\Delta y / \Delta t$ is computed from
  **coupled differential/difference equations and conservation ratios over the
  live period-$t$ integer snapshot** (e.g., police union leverage at period $t$
  is a function of current unrest to be suppressed, coercive capacity, and share
  of the municipal budget; within-class S-curve crossings are read directly off
  the heterogeneous wealth distribution without any `sigmoid()`).
- The 60 GB empirical data trove (`/media/user/data/babylon-data/` and
  `marxist-data-3NF.sqlite`) is used **offline** to deduce the flow matrices and
  lookup tables (`< 1 MB` at runtime in fixed-point/integer math—zero Spark
  cluster, zero float drift).

### The `33% / 33% / 33% / 0%` Wealth Partition (`class-dynamics.rst` + `research/14`)

Validated across both FRED Distributional Financial Accounts (60 quarters,
2010–2024) and the World Inequality Database (`piketty/`, 1820–2024):

| Population Tier | Pop Share | FRED Stock Mean | Share of `$101.4T` New Wealth Flow (2010–2024) | Babylon Class (`dim_wealth_class`) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Top 1%** | 1% | 30.1% (WID: 34.8%) | **32.6%** (`+$33.05T`) | `core_bourgeoisie` |
| **Next 9% (90–99%)** | 9% | 38.7% (WID: 34.8%) | **34.5%** (`+$35.01T`) | `petty_bourgeoisie` |
| **Next 40% (50–90%)** | 40% | 29.7% (WID: 29.5%) | **29.3%** (`+$29.67T`) | `labor_aristocracy` |
| **Bottom 50%** | 50% | 1.5% (WID: 1.0%) | **3.6%** (`+$3.67T`) | `internal_proletariat` |

- **The thirds are the stationary distribution of the flow** (`32.6% / 34.5% /
  29.3% / 3.6%`), and inverting the WID 100-percentile ladder shows that the
  **top 0.96%** owns the top third of wealth, the **next 7.8%** owns the middle
  third, and the **bottom 91.3%** shares the bottom third.
- **Homeostatic restoring force vs. bifurcation:** Inside the normal basin of
  attraction, second-order restoring forces snap perturbations right back to
  homeostasis (just as Q1 2020 stimulus pushed the Top 1% from `30.5%` to
  `29.1%` and the Bottom 50% from `1.9%` to `2.7%`, only for the system to snap
  back to `30.5%` by Q4 2020). Ordinary reforms inside the basin get eaten by
  the restoring force; only pushing a contradiction across a **bifurcation
  boundary** (cracking $\Phi$, severing circulation, or breaking the coercive
  control ratio) flips the system into a new basin.

## 9. International Trade, Blocs & Cracking $\Phi$ from the Belly of the Beast (`research/15`)

- **Abstracted into Regional Trade Blocs + Four Standalone Geopolitical Actors:**
  The player is never playing as Canada or Botswana. Outside U.S. borders, the
  world is represented as **regional Trade Blocs** (`NORTH_STAR.md`'s 12 finite
  external markets: Latin America, EU/Western Core, Sub-Saharan Africa,
  South/Southeast Asia, USMCA/Border, etc.) plus **four explicit standalone
  geopolitical actors**: **China** (whose semi-peripheral wage/OCC upgrade
  squeezes $\Phi$), **Russia**, **Iran**, and **Israel**.
  *(Future expansion pack idea logged, strictly out of v1 scope: "Babylon:
  Delinking" — playing as the People's Insurgency of Botswana on the other end
  of the imperial rent pipe.)*
- **Israel as America's Rowdy Settler-Colonial Franchise:**
  The causal arrow runs strictly from **U.S. imperialism ("daddy USA") $\rightarrow$
  Israel (its Jewish colonial franchise / forward garrison)**, never the reverse
  ("Israel does not control America"). Because a settler-colonial franchise has
  its own expansionist contradictions, it acts as America's rowdy settler
  child—periodically defying Washington's tactical restraint, starting regional
