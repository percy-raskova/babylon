## Part I — The NSA: "The Eyes and Ears in the Ether"

### 1.1 Pure observation, zero boots on the ground

The NSA has **zero arrest power, zero street agents, and zero kinetic instruments** in its `mandate`. Its
only output is **graph resolution on digital edges** and **referrals** to other cells.

Every edge on Babylon's Topology graph carries a communication medium tag (`EdgeMedium`: `Digital`,
`FinancialWire`, `Courier`, `FaceToFace`, `Printed`).

- On `Digital` and `FinancialWire` edges, the NSA's observation ceiling is **near-total (`0.95`)** across all
  50 states simultaneously. If a player coordinates chapters via unencrypted group chats, electronic dues,
  or public social media, the NSA resolves the entire connected component in one period.
- On `Courier` and `FaceToFace` edges, the NSA's observation ceiling is **`0.00`**. A handwritten note
  carried on a Greyhound bus or a conversation at a union hall is physically outside the NSA's sensorium.

### 1.2 The "Parallel Construction" Referral Bottleneck

Because the NSA cannot kick down a door, it can only monetize its dossier `L_NSA` by issuing a
**`ParallelConstructionReferral`** to the FBI, DEA, JSOC, or City PD.

- **The catch:** To protect its classification and sources, the NSA **strips provenance** when it hands a
  target node to the FBI or City PD. The receiving agency gets a target coordinate without courtroom-admissible
  probable cause, forcing the receiving cell to either:
  1. Spend its own `SURVEIL` budget to reconstruct a legal pretext ("parallel construction"), delaying the
     strike by 1–2 periods, or
  2. Strike blind on the NSA's word — which fails in court (`PROSECUTE` collapses) or hits a spoofed decoy.
- **Player counterplay:**
  1. *Analog discipline (Lenin's `Iskra` courier network):* Switching core cadre coordination from `Digital`
     to `Courier` / `FaceToFace` costs tempo (slower reach across counties, Debord's communication relay
     friction), but turns Fort Meade's screen pitch-black.
  2. *Digital Chaff / Spoofed Hubs:* The player can deliberately spin up noisy digital front groups (`Digital`
     edges with high traffic and zero cadre inside). The NSA's volume-sorted algorithm flags the spoofed hub
     as the movement's nerve center, claims credit for the intercept in the Federal Appropriation pool, and
     hands the empty warehouse to the FBI or Air Force to raid.

## Part II — The CIA: "The Shady Guy Who Does Unspeakable Things"

### 2.1 Why Langley comes home (`Operation CHAOS` $\rightarrow$ Domestic Phoenix & Gladio)

Legally, the National Security Act bars the CIA from "internal security functions." Materially, as soon as

loses confidence in the FBI and electoral co-optation), Langley activates its domestic cutout network
under a "foreign nexus" pretext (historically attested in **Operation CHAOS**, **Project MERRIMAC**,
**Project RESISTANCE**, and the domestic legs of **MKULTRA** and the **Contra-cocaine pipeline**).

Unlike the FBI (which needs an indictment) or the Army (which needs a deployment order and logistics line),
the CIA operates through **cutouts, shell foundations, private military contractors, and organized-crime /
paramilitary proxies**.

### 2.2 The CIA's Four-Mode Dirty Repertoire

Each mode is a typed `Candidate` in the CIA cell's `mandate`, priced against `L_CIA` and funded out of
two pools: **Federal Black Budget** and **Off-Books Illicit Revenue** (which does not appear in congressional
appropriations unless exposed!).

  provocations. The CIA funds and arms an unhinged ultra-left focoist splinter or neo-fascist cell to commit

## Part III — The US Department of War: The Fractal Behemoth Comes Home

When the player's Base Areas cause the **QRF arithmetic to fail in two or more domestic regions simultaneously**

- **Conservation Law #1 (The Global Boomerang):** Military force is finite and conserved. Every Active Duty

  $\Phi$ begins to collapse at home.**
