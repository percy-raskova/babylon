Play the Wayne organizer loop
=============================

Acknowledge Babylon's opening warning, then watch or skip the production card.
Choose **New Game** on the Liberty start menu. This creates a separate Wayne
organizer campaign and opens the organizer workspace. Choose **Load Game** to
return to a saved campaign later. **Continue** enters the campaign already
prepared or reopened by the launcher.

The interface grows with the game window, reaching 125% at 1920×1080.
For larger text and controls, open **Settings** and press **U** to switch the
interface size from **Automatic** to **Larger**.
Resize the game window without changing your desktop resolution.

Settings also controls audio and reduced motion. The start menu loops
**The Purge**. **Next in-game track** selects the music for the campaign.

You direct the fictional **Wayne Organizing Collective**. Its workplace
committee and neighborhood contact group are independent partners. The scenario
classifies all three groups and their time commitments as Designed. They do
not represent observed organizations or the whole industrial workforce.

Begin with the routine
----------------------

Read **Our organization** for the available time and authorized neighborhood
routine. The collective has 16 organizer-hours per period. Contact work uses
8 hours. An inquiry uses 12. A participating partner spends 2 of its own
8 hours. Unused time expires.

Choose **Hold current course**, then **Review selected approach**. Review
the cost, partner requirements and resolving period. **Confirm organizational
ruling** submits that choice. The interface calls it accepted only after the
runtime acknowledges its durable commitment. Advance one period to resolve it.

The authorized routine also runs when you advance without a specific ruling.
A specific inquiry or workplace contact replaces it for one period. Afterward,
the saved routine resumes if eligible. **Pause routine** is a separate ruling
that stays in force until you resume it.

Review the cost and confirm the ruling. Resumption performs the routine for

Respond to the workplace
------------------------

Advance until an attributed report describes reduced performed work. Read
**Workplace evidence** and its period and source before choosing a response.
The report concerns modeled labor-hours and production. Shift schedules and
wage losses are not modeled.

Compare the choices:

* **Inquiry: work / output** seeks a report of performed workplace work and
  output for the completed period.
* **Inquiry: maintenance** seeks the workplace's received, used and expired
  maintenance service. It cannot disclose the provider's private accounts.
* **Reinforce contact** performs accountability work through the existing
  workplace relationship. Mutual participation creates a contact receipt.
* **Hold current course** continues the authorized neighborhood practice.
  It does not silently pause that practice.

Use **Relationships** and **Direction / disagreements** to inspect existing
agreements, participant promises, concerns, objections and review conditions.
Your ruling decides your organization's direction. Another actor can refuse,
fail to respond, or lack enough time for its response.

Notes and the selected approach remain a personal draft while you inspect
evidence. Editing notes does not submit or execute an action. Once accepted,
a ruling is immutable and resolves with the next period.

In **Workplace evidence**, use **Previous report** and **Next report** to select
an acquired report, then **Keep report in draft**. **Open saved references**
returns to those specific reports. **Remove saved reference** removes the
personal reminder without deleting earned knowledge. After the personal draft
saves, its references and selected report survive inspection and restart. They
never change a ruling. The workspace marks edits that are waiting to save.

A reference opens a report only if you can access it in the inspected period.
Use **Return Live** to read a report acquired after that period. A retained
reference grants no access to an unavailable report.

Read consequences and continue
------------------------------

After advancing, open **Practice receipts / history**. Distinguish your
performed practice, partner response, earned knowledge, cooperation and the
next routine from factory conditions. Political choices do not repair the
factory. Its recovery follows the maintenance economy.

During the tick, the ruling panel shows **Resolving**. It credits no outcome
before the period commits. Read the completed receipt after resolution.

Initial report sharing lasts through period 3. Contact completed in period T
can sustain the same agreement for T+1 and T+2 only when the following period
consumes its receipt. Advance again and inspect the agreement and new report
access. Expiry stops automatic reports. You can use the existing contact to
try to restore cooperation.

Circuit, held history and Archive show initially disclosed knowledge and
subsequently earned evidence. An inquiry reveals one period's report, not
future accounts. Older
reports remain readable with their observed and acquisition periods. Follow
their citations and return to the same workplace workspace to review another
choice.

Held history filters reports and practice receipts. Relationships and
Direction explicitly describe the current organization. Return to the current
period and refresh the preview before submitting.

Close and reopen the saved campaign to check a pending ruling or its
completed receipt. The runtime reconciles saved commitments without a new
submission of your personal draft. At the finite campaign horizon, evidence
stays readable and the runtime refuses new rulings.

Unsupported older personal-draft formats remain on disk without conversion or
overwrite. The workspace explains when saving the personal draft is unavailable.
This does not replace or discard the campaign's authoritative commitments.

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move focus, Enter to activate a focused control, and
the notes field's displayed editing keys for text. Scroll each column to reach
its remaining controls. Resize the game window to change its size without
changing the desktop resolution.
