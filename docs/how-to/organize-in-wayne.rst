Play the Wayne organizer loop
=============================

Acknowledge Babylon's opening warning, then watch or skip the production card.
Choose **New Game** on the Liberty start menu. This creates a separate Wayne
organizer campaign and opens the organizer workspace. Choose **Load Game** to
return to a saved campaign later. **Continue** enters the campaign already
prepared or reopened by the launcher.

The interface grows with the game window, reaching 125% at 1920×1080.
For larger text and controls, open **Settings** and press **U** to switch the
interface size from **Automatic** to **Larger**.
Resize the game window without changing your desktop resolution.

Settings also controls audio and reduced motion. The start menu loops
**The Purge**. **Next in-game track** selects the music for the campaign.

You direct the fictional **Wayne Organizing Collective**. Its workplace
committee and neighborhood contact group are independent partners. The scenario
classifies all three groups and their time commitments as Designed. They do
not represent observed organizations or the whole industrial workforce.

Read the situation
------------------

The header names your collective and shows the current period and available
time. **Our situation** gives the organization's concern and neighborhood
routine. A concern expresses the organization's direction. It does not count
as an acquired workplace report. **Workplace report** shows the latest evidence
available at the inspected period, with its source and age. **Last period**
describes the most recent completed practice.

Each of the campaign's 16 periods covers 28 days. The collective has
16 organizer-hours per period. Contact work uses 8 hours. An inquiry uses 12.
A participating partner spends 2 of its own 8 hours. Unused time expires.

The campaign opens at period 0 without a completed-period workplace report.
If you confirm an inquiry and advance from period 0, it spends
12 organizer-hours but cannot get that report. The choice card and review explain
this before confirmation. Advancing first creates a completed period that a later inquiry
can ask about. That does not guarantee that the collective receives a report.

Choose and review a practice
----------------------------

Compare the four cards. Each gives its cost, partner, effect on the routine
and resolving period. For example, select **Keep current routine**, then
**Review choice**. Check the terms before choosing **Confirm ruling**. The
interface calls the ruling accepted only after the runtime acknowledges its
durable commitment. Select **Advance one period** to resolve it.

The authorized routine also runs when you advance without a specific ruling.
A specific inquiry or workplace contact replaces it for one period. Afterward,
the saved routine resumes if eligible. To change the routine itself, open
**Direction / routine**. **Pause routine** selects a separate ruling and
returns you to the decision for review. Confirming it pauses the routine from
the resolving period until you explicitly resume it.

Respond to the workplace
------------------------

Read **Workplace report** or open **Workplace evidence** before choosing a
response. Check the observed period and source. Reports concern modeled
labor-hours, production and received maintenance. Shift schedules and wage
losses are not modeled.

Compare the choices:

* **Ask about work and output** seeks the workplace's completed-period labor-hours
  and output.
* **Ask about maintenance** seeks the workplace's received, used and expired
  maintenance service. It cannot disclose the provider's private accounts.
* **Reinforce workplace contact** attempts accountability work through the existing
  workplace relationship. Mutual participation creates a contact receipt.
* **Keep current routine** continues the neighborhood practice if authorized.
  It keeps a paused routine paused.

An inquiry confirmed at period 5 asks about period 5 and resolves during
period 6. Its report does not describe period 6. Review shows both periods.
The partner's participation and receipt of a report are separate results.

Use **Relationships** and **Direction / routine** to inspect existing
agreements, participant promises, concerns, objections and review conditions.
Your ruling decides your organization's direction. Partners control their
participation. Read their recorded response separately from the evidence obtained.

The Designed scenario gives both partners a fixed policy: take part. Their time
and eligibility still apply. The broader response rules do not create policy
changes in this scenario.

Open **Personal notes** to edit your draft. Notes and the selected approach
remain personal while you inspect evidence. Editing notes does not submit or
execute an action. Once accepted, a ruling is immutable and resolves with the
next period.

In **Workplace evidence**, use **Previous report** and **Next report** to select
an acquired report, then **Keep report in draft**. In **Personal notes**,
**Open saved references** returns to those specific reports.
**Remove saved reference** removes the personal reminder without deleting earned
knowledge. After the personal draft saves, its references and selected report
survive inspection and restart. They never change a ruling. The workspace marks
edits that are waiting to save.

A reference opens a report only if you can access it in the inspected period.
Use **Return Live** to read a report acquired after that period. A retained
reference grants no access to an unavailable report.

Read consequences and continue
------------------------------

After advancing, read **Last period**. It identifies an accepted ruling or the
saved routine, time spent, the independent partner's response and the report
or contact obtained. **No report obtained** does not mean that the partner
refused: an opening-period inquiry can receive participation without obtaining
a report. Open **Practice history** for earlier receipts.

Distinguish organizational results from factory conditions. Political choices
do not repair the factory. Its recovery follows the maintenance economy.

During the tick, the ruling panel shows **Resolving**. It credits no outcome
before the period commits. Read the completed receipt after resolution.

Initial report sharing lasts through period 3. An automatic workplace report
appears only when performed labor-hours fall and workplace sharing remains
active. It describes that reduction. A full report does not appear every period.

Contact completed in period T can sustain that partner's agreement for T+1 and
T+2 when the following period consumes its receipt. Neighborhood contact renews
the neighborhood agreement. It does not renew workplace report sharing. Use
**Reinforce workplace contact** to try that contact, then advance again and
inspect **Relationships**. Expiry stops automatic workplace reports. Contact
through the existing workplace relationship can restore the agreement even
after it expires.

In the organizer workspace, **Evidence [P]** opens workplace evidence. Held
history and Archive keep the knowledge available at the inspected period.
An inquiry can reveal one period's report, not future accounts. Older reports
keep their observed and acquisition periods. Follow **Cited workplace
Archive** or **Our practice Archive** for the cited records, then use
**Back to this decision** to return to the organizer workspace.

Held history filters reports and practice receipts. Relationships and
Direction explicitly describe the current organization. Return to the current
period and refresh the preview before submitting.

Close and reopen the saved campaign to check a pending ruling or its
completed receipt. The runtime reconciles saved commitments without a new
submission of your personal draft. At the finite campaign horizon, evidence
stays readable and the runtime refuses new rulings.

Unsupported older personal-draft formats remain on disk without conversion or
overwrite. The workspace explains when saving the personal draft is unavailable.
This does not replace or discard the campaign's authoritative commitments.

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move focus, Enter to activate a focused control, and
the notes field's displayed editing keys for text. **Return to decision** or
**Escape** closes a detail view. Scroll the situation or detail view when its
contents do not fit. A hint below each panel shows when more content lies above
or below. The decision controls remain below the situation and choice cards.
Resize the game window to change its size without changing the
desktop resolution.
