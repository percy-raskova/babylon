Play the Wayne organizer loop
=============================

Acknowledge Babylon's opening warning, then watch or skip the production card.
Choose **New Game · Wayne in the national world** on the Liberty start menu.
This creates a national campaign and opens the Wayne organizer workspace.

Wayne's households and workplaces share production, trade and freight with
other counties and foreign counterparts. A sourcing policy does not guarantee
that goods arrive or that a payment settles. The separately labeled Michigan
organizer control does not offer household aid or collection.
Choose **Load Game** to return to a saved campaign later. **Continue** enters the campaign already
prepared or reopened by the launcher.

During play, choose **Menu** or press Escape to open the campaign menu.
Choose **Resume campaign** or press Escape to return to the same view.

**Settings** opens the presentation and sound controls. Escape returns from
Settings to the campaign menu. Choose **Main menu** to reach **Load Game**
or **New Game**. **Quit game** closes the game.
Babylon saves completed periods automatically.

The interface grows with the game window, reaching 125% at 1920×1080.
For larger text and controls, open **Settings** and press **U** to switch the
interface size from **Automatic** to **Larger**.
Resize the game window without changing your desktop resolution.

Settings also controls audio and reduced motion. The start menu loops
**The Purge**. **Next in-game track** selects the music for the campaign.

You direct the fictional **Wayne Organizing Collective**. Its workplace
committee and neighborhood contact group are independent partners. The scenario
classifies all three groups and their time commitments as Designed. They do
not represent observed organizations or the whole industrial workforce.

Read the situation
------------------

The header names your collective and shows the current period and available
time. **Our situation** gives the organization's concern and neighborhood
routine. A concern expresses the organization's direction. It does not count
as an acquired workplace report. **Workplace report** shows the latest evidence
available at the inspected period, with its source and age. **Last period**
describes the most recent completed practice.

Each campaign period covers 28 days. The campaign has no designed final period.
The header shows the collective's available organizer-hours, bounded by its
participants' actual household time and authorized commitments. Each choice
card shows its captured cost. A participating partner uses its own authorized
time. Unused time expires.

The campaign opens at period 0 without a completed-period workplace report.
If you confirm an inquiry and advance from period 0, it asks about a period
without a completed report. It can spend its captured hours if actual authorized
time is available, without obtaining the report. The choice card and review
explain this before confirmation. Advancing first creates a completed period
that a later inquiry can ask about. That does not guarantee that the collective
receives a report.

Choose and review a practice
----------------------------

Compare the available cards. Each gives its cost, partner, effect on the routine
and resolving period. For example, select **Keep current routine**, then
**Review choice**. Check the terms before choosing **Confirm ruling**. The
interface calls the ruling accepted only after the runtime acknowledges its
durable commitment. Select **Advance one period** to resolve it.

The authorized routine also runs when you advance without a specific ruling.
A specific inquiry or workplace contact replaces it for one period. Afterward,
the saved routine resumes if eligible. To change the routine itself, open
**Direction / routine**. **Pause routine** selects a separate ruling and
returns you to the decision for review. Confirming it pauses the routine from
the resolving period until you explicitly resume it.

Respond to the workplace
------------------------

Read **Workplace report** or open **Workplace evidence** before choosing a
response. Check the observed period and source. Reports concern modeled
labor-hours, production and received maintenance. These reports do not disclose
private household or workplace wage accounts.

Compare the choices:

* **Ask about work and output** seeks the workplace's completed-period labor-hours
  and output.
* **Ask about maintenance** seeks the workplace's received, used and expired
  maintenance service. It cannot disclose the provider's private accounts.
* **Reinforce workplace contact** attempts accountability work through the existing
  workplace relationship. Mutual participation creates a contact receipt.
* **Keep current routine** continues the neighborhood practice if authorized.
  It keeps a paused routine paused.

An inquiry confirmed at period 5 asks about period 5 and resolves during
period 6. Its report does not describe period 6. Review shows both periods.
The partner's participation and receipt of a report are separate results.

Fund aid and compare solidarity choices
---------------------------------------

**Collect a voluntary contribution** names the contributor, its independent
cash consent and the committed period at which the runtime verified the terms.
The scenario authors the mandate and its source as Designed content. Authority
over the collective, a time pledge or a received gift grants no permission to
spend another household's cash.

The cap is an upper bound. Actual household consumption, essential services,
closing pantry, due payments and the protected cash floor come first. A smaller
available amount can produce a partial contribution.

A positive contribution uses the full captured collection hours once. Collecting less cash does not
create spare hours. A refused collection transfers no cash and performs no
collection work. It still resolves the original accepted ruling, which replaces
the routine for that period.

Review reserves no funds or time. Advance closes the material period and reports
the amount actually received. That money can fund a later aid ruling. It does
not grant membership, partner participation or more hours.

Compare **Organize local aid** with **Organize remote solidarity**. Both protect
the donor's food needs and must use real organizational cash, finite household
work and independent receiving consent. Local aid can arrive in its dispatch
period. Remote solidarity shares freight capacity with ordinary trade. Its
earliest possible arrival is a preview, not a promise. Follow the original
shipment through arrival or failure before interpreting the result.

Use **Last period** and **Practice history** to read the actual collection
result. The eight recent practice receipts retain exact collection amounts
and outcomes after a later period or reopen. Use the cited practice Archive
for older receipts. In **Practice history**, distinguish reserved aid cash,
actual gift payment, refunds, dispatched food, consumption and later
coordination. Receiving
a gift does not compel political participation. A declined practice does not
revoke delivered food. Compare these choices with inquiry, workplace contact
and retaining the routine: each uses time that can
serve another practice.

Use **Relationships** and **Direction / routine** to inspect existing
agreements, participant promises, concerns, objections and review conditions.
Your ruling decides your organization's direction. Partners control their
participation. Read their recorded response separately from the evidence obtained.

The Designed scenario gives both partners a fixed policy: take part. Their time
and eligibility still apply. The broader response rules do not create policy
changes in this scenario.

Open **Personal notes** to edit your draft. Notes and the selected approach
remain personal while you inspect evidence. Editing notes does not submit or
execute an action. Once accepted, a ruling is immutable and resolves with the
next period.

In **Workplace evidence**, use **Previous report** and **Next report** to select
an acquired report, then **Keep report in draft**. In **Personal notes**,
**Open saved references** returns to those specific reports.
**Remove saved reference** removes the personal reminder without deleting earned
knowledge. After the personal draft saves, its references and selected report
survive inspection and restart. They never change a ruling. The workspace marks
edits that are waiting to save.

A reference opens a report only if you can access it in the inspected period.
Use **Return Live** to read a report acquired after that period. A retained
reference grants no access to an unavailable report.

Take a household to the county review
--------------------------------------

The county holds a review session every period from period 4, after that period's accounts close.
It ranks households by the wage tax each paid in the period just closed and pays relief down that
list. A household that pays more than a household holding a unit of relief can take the unit. Your
organizer can reach one household in a named position, from period 3 on. That household asks the
county for a unit. This slice has no nation, no solidarity and no consciousness. Households act on
their own money only.

Read the county before you choose:

#. Open **County ledger**. It shows the county's cash, its spending limit, the lines it pays before
   relief and the money for relief. No claim can touch the lines paid before relief.
#. Open **Household roster**. **Who works** counts, for each band of households, those with someone
   at work, those only looking for work and those with nobody in the labor force. The bands are set
   at the opening and are Designed. Below them, the positions follow the county's list.
#. Read a position's line. It gives its band, budget group, workplace and how that workplace pays,
   the people at work and looking for work, the units it holds and its rank. A position you have not
   contacted shows no dollar figures: **Amounts are earned by contact**. A position with no
   workplace has nobody in the labor force and no wage, so it can take nothing from anyone.

Reach a household:

#. Use **Previous** and **Next** to find a position. Eight positions show on a page.
#. Press **Reach a household here (N)** for the position you choose. The roster closes and the
   choice is drafted. On a past period the buttons are off and the roster says why. Choose
   **Return Live** first.
#. Choose **Review choice**. The review costs two organizer hours, names the period the contact
   lands in and the period the preview is worked from, and says one of these: a place is open and
   whose unit it would take; no place is open; nobody is left to reach; the position is no longer on
   the roster; or a reached household here would not ask.
#. Choose **Confirm ruling**, then **Advance one period**.

The contact is carried out when the next period closes, like every practice. Open **County session**
after the close. It gives the money for relief, the units payable and kept back, and **What this
session changed**: which positions asked and were paid, which units went unpaid and why a reached
household stayed home. The unit a household wins is paid when the period after the session closes.
It holds for that period only, so the household has to come again.

Each household that comes brings one more household of its own position, who is reached from the
next session. A claim therefore doubles each session, until the position has no household left to
reach or no unit is left below it. It then stops at the edge of its position. To go further, reach a
household in another position. You cannot call back a household you have reached.

A period spent at the county is a period not spent on food. **Food run** shows the parcels the
organization's households can spare and the last local aid run. When the county's cash falls below
the lines it pays first, it announces no relief, and nobody is paid whatever their place.

Every session adds its public record to the organization's page in the Archive: the money for
relief, the units paid and unpaid and the first positions on the list. The organization's page for
a period carries that period's session and no other. **County session** shows the session of the
period you are inspecting. To read an earlier session, choose **Previous period** until you reach
it. **County session** then says the session of that period is in the Archive. Open **Our practice
Archive** for the same period to read it. Choose **Return Live** before you choose again.

**Play** stops for a session only when it is worth reading, and says why in the notice. It stops
at the county's first session, "The county's session record arrived." It stops at a session that
carries your contact, "The county's session record arrived: your contact was heard." It stops at
a session that changes how the county's money stands against the roll: "the county could not pay
every unit" when it paid every unit and now pays some, "the county paid no household unit" when it
now pays none, whether it paid every unit or some before, and "the county can pay every unit
again" when it pays every unit after a session that did not. Each of these follows "The county's
session record arrived:". Any other session installs without a pause: read it in **County
session** when you want to.

Read consequences and continue
------------------------------

After advancing, read **Last period**. It identifies an accepted ruling or the
saved routine, time spent, the independent partner's response and the report
or contact obtained. **No report obtained** does not mean that the partner
refused: an opening-period inquiry can receive participation without obtaining
a report. Open **Practice history** for earlier receipts.

Distinguish organizational results from factory conditions. Political choices
do not repair the factory. Its recovery follows the maintenance economy.

During the tick, the ruling panel shows **Resolving**. It credits no outcome
before the period commits. Read the completed receipt after resolution.

**Relationships** shows the period through which workplace report sharing
remains active. An automatic workplace report appears only when performed
labor-hours fall and that sharing remains active. It describes that reduction.
A full report does not appear every period.

Contact completed in period T can sustain that partner's agreement when the
following period consumes its receipt. The captured renewal duration determines
how many following periods it covers. Inspect **Relationships** for its endpoint.

Neighborhood contact renews the neighborhood agreement. It does not renew
workplace report sharing. Use **Reinforce workplace contact** to try that
contact, then advance again and
inspect **Relationships**. Expiry stops automatic workplace reports. Contact
through the existing workplace relationship can restore the agreement even
after it expires.

In the organizer workspace, **Evidence [P]** opens workplace evidence. Held
history and Archive keep the knowledge available at the inspected period.
An inquiry can reveal one period's report, not future accounts. Older reports
keep their observed and acquisition periods. Follow **Cited workplace
Archive** or **Our practice Archive** for the cited records, then use
**Back to this decision** to return to the organizer workspace.

Held history filters reports and practice receipts. Relationships and
Direction explicitly describe the current organization. Return to the current
period and refresh the preview before submitting.

Close and reopen the saved campaign to check a pending ruling or its
completed receipt. The runtime reconciles saved commitments without a new
submission of your personal draft. Continuous campaigns accept later rulings.
An explicit finite experiment stops at its saved endpoint, where evidence stays
readable and the runtime refuses new rulings.

The runtime refuses campaigns captured under an incompatible organizer format
before writes. Their saved data remains on disk. Start a fresh campaign for the
current collection rules.

Unsupported older personal-draft formats remain on disk without conversion or
overwrite. The workspace explains when saving the personal draft is unavailable.
This does not replace or discard the campaign's authoritative commitments.

Use Tab and Shift+Tab to move focus, Enter to activate a focused control, and
the notes field's displayed editing keys for text. **Return to decision** or
**Escape** closes a detail view. Scroll the situation or detail view when its
contents do not fit. A hint below each panel shows when more content lies above
or below. The decision controls remain below the situation and choice cards.
Resize the game window to change its size without changing the
desktop resolution.
