# First State Opponent Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use `superpowers:subagent-driven-development` task by task. Track steps with checkboxes and coordinate separate worktrees and heavy gates.

**Goal:** Deliver the approved City PD witness as an independently acting, funded, knowledge-limited opponent in the real national campaign, through T+1 resolution, restart and a player-visible receipt.

**Architecture:** Capture one Designed agency, its real account and bounded case inputs alongside the national foundation. Generate its next-period intent from its own committed knowledge; execute the paid observation in the existing detached material tick, persist its private state with the canonical register, and project only witnessed facts through the organizer and Archive. The county-scoped public account is an explicitly Designed fiscal proxy, not a city treasury.

**Tech stack:** Rust, BSL, canonical binary codecs, PostgreSQL, existing Bevy organizer and Archive.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-29-first-state-opponent.md`; authority and exact political footprint: `ai/decisions/ADR291_first_state_opponent_observation.yaml`.

## Global constraints

- This is partial PER-355; do not assert 14-cell acceptance or change the full Goal.
- One 28-day detached tick, next-period material effects, marker-last commit, no hidden-truth decision input, no new primitive or response curve, no direct coercion.
- All fictional agency, lead, witness, prioritization and staffing bindings are Designed; a sourced wage must carry its actual provenance and vintage.
- Keep one monetary principal: `Capacity` is a temporary allocation view of actual unreserved agency cash, never an independently persisted duplicate balance.
- Preserve household/cohort and financial totals. Existing historical records and unsupported old campaigns remain recoverable, but incompatible active formats refuse before mutation; no compatibility migration.
- Add the exact native intent allowance only with its real effect. Do not add unused permission rows.
- Every task starts with a meaningful failing test, makes it pass, then refactors. Each worker owns the listed files; integrate dependencies before downstream writes.
- Run scoped tests first; serialize heavy Rust and PostgreSQL gates. Do not generate documentation.

## Review focus

- Unknown edge between two known nodes must not alter an agency decision (Task 1).
- A valid case with no remaining public fact can cost time and yield no fact; it cannot guarantee discovery (Tasks 2 and 4).
- A partially funded appropriation, a later-period tax payment and competing household hours must not create funds or time (Task 3).
- An ambiguous commit or restart cannot duplicate the visit, payroll or player receipt (Task 5).
- A non-witness must not infer an unseen subject, case count, refusal or graph shape from a player surface (Task 6).
