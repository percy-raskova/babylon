## Findings that change the implementation

### Reproduction replaces its own conditions

The Grundrisse Introduction treats production, distribution, exchange and
consumption as differentiated moments of a connected social process. Capital
II's reproduction sections require both the appropriate material forms and
their circulation between uses. A stock of money or accumulated finished goods
is not by itself a reproduced productive circuit.

**Implementation:** recurrent household needs/purchases/consumption and recurrent
procurement must replace finite opening orders. Means of production and means of
consumption describe uses of output; they are not a forced two-sector NAICS map.
Opening working capital, pantry stocks and resource reserves must be visible.

**Qualification:** reproduction equalities under stated assumptions are useful
accounting controls. They do not license a market-clearing equation that forces
every produced commodity to find a buyer.

### Need, demand and realization can separate

Grundrisse Notebook IV distinguishes production from realization and treats the
world market as an expansion of the circuit with its own limits. Capital I's
labor-power discussion includes historically developed subsistence requirements,
dependants and reproduction. Workers' needs do not disappear when firms stop
hiring. Wage expenditure is simultaneously an employer outlay and a source of
household purchasing power.

**Implementation:** preserve need, desired purchase, affordable order, dispatch,
arrival, consumption, wage due and wage paid separately. Record failed purchases
and unsold goods. Fixed population and participation are explicit controls;
QCEW jobs do not become an unaccounted source of persons.

**Qualification:** this establishes causal questions, not a source-derived
household utility curve or a claim that every household has identical needs.

### Money, capital and investment are not interchangeable stocks

Capital II chapter 2 distinguishes accumulated money from its conversion into
expanded productive capital. Grundrisse Notebook VII makes equipment,
maintenance, circulating inputs and future reproduction commitments material
conditions. Amin's two-department example changes equipment when productivity
changes; unchanged machines do not simply become more capable.

**Implementation:** retained earnings can fund a real order; delivery and
installation precede capacity. Record capital advanced versus consumed cost,
installed versus pending assets, and cash versus income. Preserve hoards,
unprofitable replacement and failed expansion as possible outcomes.

**Qualification:** Amin's printed 18–28 example has explicit saving, technical
and transaction-timing assumptions. Its credit discussion does not prove that
every cash-funded closed control is impossible, nor that investment generates
an unlimited outlet. The control must state its financing and settlement order.

### Profit and value analysis require named accounts and valuation dates

Capital III chapter 4 and Grundrisse pp. 745–757 distinguish turnover, profit
mass, capital advanced and rates. Foley's flow-based value-of-money argument is
not the inverse wage; sector/national money flows can diverge from value flows.
Freeman/Kliman pp. 34–35 explicitly reject equating temporal valuation with
acquisition historical cost: their treatment values inputs when consumed,
allowing changes between purchase and use without retrospectively repricing
already consumed inputs. Their value analysis is not a price-setting algorithm.

**Implementation:** retain acquisition, use, payment and explicit revaluation
facts separately. The initial operating ledger uses named transaction costs;
do not call it TSSI. Define numerator, denominator, period and valuation
convention for any profit measure. Use the same physical transactions when
comparing interpretations. Prices need their own inspectable Designed policy.

**Qualification:** neither wages above a GDP/hour ratio nor a county's revenue
automatically establishes class or value production. Do not force a falling
profit rate, crisis, price-value convergence or political trajectory.

Capital I chapters 7–9 further distinguish the exercise of labor-power from its
purchase, and socially necessary labor from wasted time under abnormal
production conditions. Paid idle time can be a real employer cost without
creating goods or new value. Surplus labor described as unpaid in value theory
does not mean a wage obligation was left unpaid. Capital III chapter 17 treats
commercial employees as wage workers while distinguishing pure circulation work
from direct surplus-value production. Productive-labor classification alone
cannot establish a person's class position.

Capital II's storage argument distinguishes stocks needed for continuity from
unsold accumulation caused by blocked realization. Both can use preservation
work and materials, but extra costs do not guarantee a recoverable price. A
sale to a wholesaler can realize a manufacturer's capital while leaving the
goods unsold socially. Track resale margins without counting the same output
as repeated new production. Capital III's equalized profit rates belong to its
analytical assumptions; the game must not force those rates to equality.

### Imperial relations require more than a domestic production diagram

Lenin's capital-export and parasitism chapters concern ownership, monopolistic
advantages and differentiated social interests as well as commodity trade.
MIM's productive-labor and superprofit arguments distinguish wage employment,
productive labor and exploitation. Amin printed 86–94 distinguishes workers,
peasants and bourgeoisies within center and periphery. Cope printed 160–162
addresses possible overlap between unequal-exchange estimates and remitted
profits, while acknowledging incomplete empirical disaggregation.

**Implementation:** foreign production and domestic consumption compete for
finite goods, labor and purchasing power. Represent owners and remittance
counterparties separately from plant location. A paid remittance transfers
existing funds once; a theoretical appropriation report cannot create another
cash flow. Nationality, trade bloc, class, ownership and conceived interests
remain different relations.

**Qualification:** twelve external markets are Designed compression. The cited
1990s/2010s estimates are not calibrated 2024 parameters. Neither Amin's wage
zones nor Cope's book provides a ready-made contemporary Russia/China class
classifier. A closed control cannot establish the adequacy of the full
imperialism model.

### Paid, unpaid, productive and commercial work require separate treatment

Capital II's transport section and Cope printed 176–182 distinguish physical
transport continuing production from pure commercial circulation. Cope printed
182–185 discusses unpaid domestic work and petty production in reproduction.
MC5's 1998 gender article, lines 196–237, recognizes cooking, cleaning and other
unpaid work but disputes defining gender through reproduction, emphasizing
class and imperial relations. These arguments contain disagreements; they must
not be represented as a single consensus theory of gender.

**Implementation:** declare finite paid/unpaid time, goods and service
requirements. Goods received can relieve a material constraint on an already
accounted person's contribution. They cannot create extra hours, consent,
membership or consciousness. Do not infer unpaid work or class from sex or
nationality. Freight prices, commercial margins and value creation are separate
quantities; paid usefulness alone is not a productive-labor classification.

### Investigation must test particular relations

On Practice links knowledge to social practice and repeated correction. On
Contradiction §§I–IV requires attention to particular processes, stages and
relations; external conditions operate through internal relations. Sections V–VI
distinguish conditioned transformation and different forms of struggle, including
the distinction between contradiction and antagonism.

**Implementation:** supply interruption, cash shortage, inadequate labor and
withheld agreement must have different observable mechanisms. Test which link
binds before applying an intervention. Keep current observations, inferred
explanations and later receipts distinct. Player investigation should make a
particular decision better informed.

**Qualification:** a technical bottleneck is not automatically a social
contradiction, and a pressure scalar is not an explanation of social change.
Do not turn Mao's historical/philosophical arguments into a guaranteed game
trajectory or a universal response curve.

## Retain, revise and reject

Retain exact physical accounts, finite shared allocation, conserved workforce
pools, atomic replay, independently authorized practices and existing freight /
staffing / maintenance counterfactuals. Extend them through real consumers.

Revise finite end-buyer fulfillment into owned household stock and consumption;
physical-only planning into demand-, inventory- and finance-aware planning;
opening endowments into recurrent reproduction; and geographically attributed
profit into explicit ownership accounts.

Reject importing the historical abstract wealth-production rules as this
circuit, treating money as goods, adding trade and remittance estimates as
separate new money, equating observed payroll with value produced, or interpreting
a timestamped cost ledger as proof of an entire valuation school.
