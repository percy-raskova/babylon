Your first county review
========================

In this lesson you play one way through the County Review, from a new campaign to the second
session. You read the county's books. You reach one household in one position. You watch the county
pay it one unit of relief, and you see why the claim stops there.

You close five periods. Each takes about ten seconds. Most of the lesson is reading the screen.

Before you start
----------------

You need the game, and you need to reach the Liberty start menu. You do not need to know anything
about the County Review. The lesson explains each line when you reach it.

Babylon is deterministic. A new campaign opens the same way every time. The figures in this lesson
were read from that opening, in a recorded playthrough and on the game's own screens. They hold only
if you choose nothing else on the way. Press only the buttons the lesson names, in the order it
names them.

After every step the lesson says what you see. If a figure on your screen differs from the lesson,
you have left the path. Do not try to repair the campaign. Open **Menu** and choose **Main menu**,
then begin again at step 1 from the Liberty start menu.

If the household roster says "Past period: reading only", or a county view begins "These figures are
period", you have stepped back to an earlier period. Open **Time** and press **Return Live**.

Two things on the screen need a word before you begin.

* A line marked **Designed** is a designed input. The designers set it. It is not a measurement of
  Wayne County.
* The slice you play is small on purpose. Its own screens say so: "This slice has no nation, no
  solidarity and no consciousness. Households act on their own money only."

Step 1. Start the campaign
--------------------------

Acknowledge the opening warning, then watch or skip the production card. On the Liberty start
menu, choose **New Game · Wayne in the national world**.

The game opens the Wayne organizer workspace. You see:

* the header **Wayne Organizing Collective**, with the line "P0 committed" under it, and the button
  **Advance one period** at the top right of the window;
* a board headed "Work for period 1";
* at the bottom right, a panel headed "Period 0 dispatch". The button **Wayne hearing** is at the
  top of it. Under that are four buttons, two to a line: **County ledger**, **Household roster**,
  **County session** and **Food run**.

Period 0 is the opening. A period is four weeks.

Step 2. Read the county ledger
------------------------------

Press **County ledger**.

You see a view headed "COUNTY LEDGER · period 0". It names the Wayne County administration. Under
the name is one line of figures:

"Cash: $344,127,900.00 · spending limit: $172,063,974.00 · one unit of relief: $280.00".

A unit of relief is the county's support for one person for one period.

Under "Lines in pay order" you see eleven lines. The first five are marked "paid before relief".
They add up to "Paid before relief: $151,969,774.00". No claim can touch them. The last six are
marked "relief". They add up to the money for relief, $20,094,200.00. Two of them matter to this
lesson:

* line 7, earning-nonowner, $10,874,360.00;
* line 11, no-earner-nonowner, $7,430,080.00.

Below the lines you see "The county has not held a session yet. Its first is in period 4." Then
"The roll by budget group" lists the units each group holds.

Press **Return to decision [Esc]**.

Step 3. Read the household roster
---------------------------------

Press **Household roster**.

You see a block headed "Who works". It has one entry for each of four bands. Each entry carries the
word Designed and a short description of the band. Under each description are the band's counts.
Check two of them:

* Top 1%: "7,006 households", 0 with someone at work, and 7,006 with nobody in the labor force.
* Next 40%: "280,236 households", all 280,236 with someone at work, and 0 with nobody in the labor
  force.

The block ends with "Bands are set at the opening. Homes and wealth are not in this slice."

Below it you see "Household positions · page 1 of 14 · 111 positions, in the order of the county's
list". A position is named by its band, its budget group and, where its households work, a
workplace. Its line also gives the people at work, the people looking for work and the units it
holds, and two positions can share a name. Under the heading you read "The county has not held a
session yet." and "The county holds its first session in period 4. A contact can be made from
period 3."

A position you have not contacted shows no dollar figures. Its entry ends with "Amounts are earned
by contact. Reach a household here."

Press **Return to decision [Esc]**.

Step 4. Read the county session
-------------------------------

Press **County session**.

You see one line: "No county session has been held as of period 0. The first is in period 4." Under
it is the line about the nation, solidarity and consciousness.

Press **Return to decision [Esc]**.

Step 5. Read the food run
-------------------------

Press **Food run**.

You see "FOOD RUN · local aid". You read that the organization's households can spare 0 parcels
(8 in stock, 8 needed at home), and that "No local aid run has resolved yet." You do not run food in
this lesson.

Press **Return to decision [Esc]**.

Step 6. Advance to period 3
---------------------------

Press **Advance one period**, at the top right of the window. Wait for the period to close, then
press it again, and again: three presses in all. Choose nothing else. The routine your organization
already has runs by itself.

While you advance, a notice at the foot of the board may mention the Wayne hearing. It may read
"Invitation window open", "Wayne hearing minutes arrived" or, when period 3 closes, "The county
review report arrived". The map may mark "County review · P3". All of these belong to the Wayne
hearing. None of them is the county's session, which holds its first in period 4. You do not need
to open the hearing, and you can advance without opening it.

When the third period closes you see "P3 committed" under the header, and the panel at the bottom
right is headed "Period 3 dispatch". Under the four county buttons it starts with "Period 3 · saved
routine".

Step 7. Read the roster again
-----------------------------

Press **Household roster**.

The counts have moved. The engine's wages fall in periods 2 and 3, and with them the households
that have someone at work. You see:

* Next 40%: 127,432 households with someone at work and 152,804 only looking for work.
* Bottom 50%: 84,634 households with someone at work, 118,200 only looking for work, and 84,648
  people at work.

The heading now reads "page 1 of 17 · 131 positions". You read "The county has not held a session
yet." The line about making a contact is gone, because you may make one now.

Step 8. Reach a household
-------------------------

Scroll the roster. The hint "More below · scroll this panel" tells you there is more. Each position
shows its line and, under it, a button **Reach a household here (N)**, with N from 1 to 8. Eight
positions show on a page, in the order of the county's list. Stay on page 1.

Find the eighth position of page 1. Its line reads:

"Bottom 50% · earning-nonowner · Wayne County: extraction (5) (wage) · 1 at work · 0 looking for
work · 0 units held".

The first seven positions cannot move a unit. Either no place is open for them or a household
reached there would not ask. The eighth is the first position on the list that can move one. You
do not need to try the others.

Press **Reach a household here (8)**.

The roster closes. The board now shows a card headed **County review contact**. It reads "Reach a
household at Bottom 50% · earning-nonowner · Wayne County: extraction (5) (wage)", costs "2 of 2
organizer-hours", and resolves in P4. The contact takes the period's two hours and replaces your
routine for that period.

Step 9. Review the choice
-------------------------

Press **Review choice**, at the top right of the board.

You see a view headed "Review the ruling". You find these lines in it:

* "Target: Bottom 50% · earning-nonowner · Wayne County: extraction (5) (wage)".
* "Cost: 2 of 2 organizer-hours".
* "The contact lands in period 4, the period of the county's next session."
* "A place is open. The unit would come from: Bottom 50% · no-earner-nonowner · Wayne County:
  distribution_transport (5) (wage)."
* "This preview is worked from period 3, the last close you have seen."

Remember the position named in the fourth line. You meet it again in step 12.

Step 10. Confirm the ruling
---------------------------

Press **Confirm ruling**, the button at the top of the review, under **Return to decision [Esc]**.

The review closes. The **County review contact** card is now marked as accepted for P4, and
**Review choice** shows UNAVAILABLE. Nothing has happened in the county yet. The contact is carried
out when the period closes.

Step 11. Advance to period 4
----------------------------

Press **Advance one period**. Wait about ten seconds.

You see "P4 committed" under the header. At the foot of the board, after "Personal draft saved", a
notice tells you that the county's session record arrived and that your contact was heard: "The
county's session record arrived: your contact was heard."

Step 12. Read the session
-------------------------

Press **County session**.

You see a view headed "COUNTY SESSION · period 4". Read it from the top.

#. "Money for relief: $20,094,200.00 · units payable: 70,993 · kept back by an exact tie: 0". The
   county has exactly the money for the units already on its roll, and no more. It is the sum of
   the six relief lines you read in step 2. When a household that was not on the roll asks and is
   paid, a unit lower on the list goes unpaid.
#. Two roll lines, one for each body in the county. A body's roll is the households that came to
   the session behind that body's claim. It is not the county's roll of units you read in step 2.
   The "Wayne Relief Claimants Assembly" line reads "0 households came from the roll". The "Wayne
   Local Aid Partners" line reads "1 household asked and was paid". That household is the one you
   reached.
#. "Your contact at the session: Bottom 50% · earning-nonowner · Wayne County: extraction (5)
   (wage) · a household was reached".
#. The block "What this session changed" has two lines.

The first line of that block begins "rank 6 · Bottom 50% · earning-nonowner · Wayne County:
extraction (5) (wage):". Rank 6 is the position's place on the county's list. The line goes on:
"1 household asked and was paid, 1 household was brought, to be reached from the next session."

One household in your position asked the county for a unit, and the county paid it. That household
brought one more household of its own position. That household will be reached from the next
session, without any further contact from you.

The second line begins "rank 7 · Bottom 50% · no-earner-nonowner · Wayne County:
distribution_transport (5) (wage):" and ends "1 unit left unpaid." This is the position the review
named in step 9. The unit the county paid your household was taken from it.

Press **Return to decision [Esc]**, then press **County ledger**.

You see "COUNTY LEDGER · period 4" and "Money for relief at the last session: $20,094,200.00". The
lines have moved by one unit, $280, for the next period:

* line 7, earning-nonowner, is now $10,874,640.00, which is $280 more;
* line 11, no-earner-nonowner, is now $7,429,800.00, which is $280 less.

The county pays the unit when period 5 closes. It holds for that period only, so the household has
to come again.

Press **Return to decision [Esc]**.

Step 13. Advance to period 5 and read why the claim stops
---------------------------------------------------------

The board is now headed "Work for period 5". It still shows the **County review contact** card,
marked "Available to review". That is a draft. A draft does nothing until you review and confirm
it. Leave it.

Press **Advance one period**. Wait about ten seconds. Choose nothing. You see "P5 committed".

Press **County session**.

You see "COUNTY SESSION · period 5". The money for relief is still $20,094,200.00 and the units
payable are still 70,993. The line "Your contact at the session" is gone, because you made no
contact in period 4. Both roll lines read 0.

Under "What this session changed" you now see one line. It begins "rank 5 · Bottom 50% ·
earning-nonowner · Wayne County: extraction (5) (wage):" and reads "2 households asked and found no
place open."

Rank 5 is now the last place on the list. Every position whose households paid no wage tax shares
it, so the smaller number is not a better place.

The two households are the one you reached and the one it brought. Both asked. No place was open
for either, and no unit moved.

The reason is the workplace. Wayne County: extraction (5) paid its workers $2,273,280 a period
through period 3, then $242,304 in period 4, and nothing from period 5. The county ranks households
by the wage tax each paid in the period that has closed. A household whose workplace pays no wage
stands at the foot of the list, and a household at the foot finds no place.

Press **Return to decision [Esc]**, then press **County ledger**. The two lines have gone back to
the figures you read in step 2: line 7 is $10,874,360.00 and line 11 is $7,430,080.00.

The claim has stopped. You made the contact once and you do not return to it. In the recorded run
the two households ask again in every later session, to its last period, 34, and each time they
find no place. That is the recorded course of this playthrough. It is not a fault.

What you have learned
---------------------

* You read the county's books: $151,969,774.00 is paid before relief and no claim can touch it;
  $20,094,200.00 is the money for relief.
* In period 3 you chose to reach one household in Bottom 50% · earning-nonowner · Wayne County:
  extraction (5) (wage). Before you confirmed, the review told you that a place was open and whose
  unit it would take.
* At the session of period 4 that household asked, was paid one unit for period 5, and brought one
  more household. The unit came from the position the review named.
* At the session of period 5 its workplace paid no wage, so both households asked and found no
  place. The county ranks households by the wage tax each paid in the period that has closed, and a
  household with no wage can take nothing from anyone.
* This claim ended because its workplace stopped paying a wage. A claim also stops when its
  position has no household left to reach or no unit is left below it.
* The slice has no nation, no solidarity and no consciousness. Households act on their own money
  only.

Where to go next
----------------

* :doc:`/how-to/organize-in-wayne` gives the general procedure for taking a household to the county
  review, and for the rest of the Wayne organizer workspace. It also explains the Archive record
  that every session adds to your organization's page.
* The recorded playthroughs are in ``reports/county-review-2026-11-19/``. Its README says what each
  record shows: no contact, one position, two positions, and food runs.
* To see a claim that grows, read the "two positions" record in the same README. In it the player
  made a second contact in period 10, in Next 40% · earning-nonowner · "Wayne County: food (5)".
  It landed in the session of period 11, and the households that came doubled in each session
  from 1 to 16 before that workplace stopped paying a wage.

The executable form of this lesson
----------------------------------

A test plays this lesson's way. The test ``county_review_playthroughs_smoke`` in
``rust/crates/babylon-persistence/tests/observer_material_live/county_review_playthroughs.rs``
sends the requests the client sends, without the game window. It plays "no contact" through period
5 and this lesson's way, "one position", through period 6. It asserts that the contact lands in the
first session after it and that a unit moves, and it replays the run. It takes several minutes and
needs the project's disposable PostgreSQL runtime and the national source data:

.. code-block:: bash

   BABYLON_POSTGRES_LIVE_FOCUS=county_review \
   BABYLON_COUNTY_REVIEW_TEST=county_review_playthroughs_smoke \
   mise run test:rust-postgres

The recorded "one position" playthrough, ``reports/county-review-2026-11-19/one-position.json``,
holds the figures of this lesson. Read its README beside it.
