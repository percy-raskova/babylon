## The Dimitrov critique (why he's out)

Dimitrov's "open terrorist dictatorship of the most reactionary, most chauvinistic and most
imperialist elements of finance capital" is rejected as vacuous and category-confused: "Is
there such a thing as a not-reactionary finance capital? … What unit of measurement are we
using to measure chauvinism? I was not aware such a thing could be quantified! I could have
sworn it was rather, a qualitative shift!" A crystallized definition cannot capture fascism's
fluctuating character.

## The Jackson frame (Blood in My Eye)

Fascism is a dialectical process in constant fluctuation between three phases:
1. **Insecure, out of power**
2. **Insecure and in power**
3. **Secure and in power**

Key dynamic: **secure-in-power fascism can PERMIT mass dissent** because it holds ideological
hegemony — the essay's example is early Mussolini Italy (press freedom, PCI in parliament, for
years). Dimitrov's definition misses this entirely; Jackson's explains it. (Sim consequence:
the phase couples to LEGITIMATION/hegemony strength, not just repression intensity — a secure
regime represses LESS visibly.)

## The materialist oppression criterion

"A social relationship can be said to be a form of oppression if, and only if, there is an
extractive transfer of value from the oppressed to the oppressor class."

## The Four Horsemen — each mode named by WHAT it extracts and WHAT it acts on

1. **Misogyny** — extracts **reproductive labor** (sexual reproduction, domestic housework,
   child rearing, education) from women-as-a-class to the bourgeoisie (and to a lesser extent
   men). Women's participation is the intersectional net-transfer calculus: "the value that
   they could extract from integration and assimilation into the war machine … outweighed the
   value extracted from them from patriarchy"; "the share of the spoils of exploitation that
   they participate in outweigh what is extracted from them" (Auschwitz guards, IDF women —
   "a stolen house built on stolen Palestinian land").
2. **Eugenics** — an ATTEMPT (emphasized: pseudoscientific, doesn't work) to **standardize
   the labor-power commodity** by eliminating "defective" workers: capitalists want
   predictable, standardized labor-power to plan maximal exploitation. Acts on the QUALITY
   DISTRIBUTION of labor-power.
3. **White supremacy** — Du Bois's **wages of whiteness**: a psychic wage (plus sometimes a
   real wage differential) that pacifies white labor via racial hierarchy while pacifying
   Black/Indigenous/non-white labor "through brutality, policing, and genocide" — a docile
   labor pool enabling accumulation. **Acts on LABOR.**
4. **Settler colonialism** — "brazen and bloodthirsty theft of land"; white workers and white
   bourgeoisie "cross the class divide and collaborate over divvying up the spoils of looted
   Native lands." **Acts on LAND** — the explicit axis split with white supremacy.

## Simulation consequences (design input, not a ruling)

- **Capture as a transfer computation.** The join criterion is the same shape as the Survival
  Calculus: fascist-capture propensity for a stratum is a NET EXPECTED TRANSFER (share of
  bloc-extracted value flowing in, minus value the bloc's internal hierarchies extract from
  the stratum) — not a fixed demographic weight. Slots directly into ADR208 R1's parameter
  rows on FASCIST_VEHICLE.
- **Entitlement gets a derivation.** The frozen `entitlement` scalar reactionary.py reads is,
  on this frame, the stored expectation of bloc-side transfers (wages of whiteness + settler
  spoils + patriarchal position) — eventually computable from the value-flow estate rather
  than seeded.
- **Labor/land axis maps to the substrate split.** White supremacy's mechanism lives on class
  nodes and wage/exploitation edges; settler colonialism's lives on territory/hex tenure —
  the engine already separates these. A fascist bloc's transfer offer is therefore a SUM over
  distinct substrate channels, not one scalar.
- **Cross-class capture is expected, not anomalous.** Settler cross-class collaboration means
  eligibility (_ENTITLED_ROLES) generalizes to any stratum with positive net transfer —
  matching Sakai's warning that fascism recruits across lines.
- **Jackson phases couple to legitimation.** The mutable capture-state flag (R1) wants a
  phase dimension whose behavior differs BY hegemony strength: secure-in-power tolerates
  visible dissent while staying extractive. The Electoral/Policy legitimation machinery is
  the natural carrier.
- **Two open gaps the essay exposes in the sim:** (a) reproductive labor is not modeled —
  misogyny's extraction channel has no current substrate home; (b) labor-power quality
  dispersion (eugenics' target) has no explicit representation beyond wealth/vitality. Both
  are Director option-space questions for the dossier, not rulings.
