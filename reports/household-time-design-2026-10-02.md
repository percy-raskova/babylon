# Household time and organizational support

This is a Designed accounting control and implementation decision, not a claim
that household time or aid already works in the national campaign. ADR270 owns
the decision. PER-337 and the approved national plan own delivery and acceptance.

Households are the smallest economic decision unit. County cohorts compress
similar accounts, while residents and workers remain quantities. The initializer
can divide a budget cohort into small participant or recipient groups. The groups
must conserve its persons, households, workforce, cash, stocks and ownership.
Participation does not define an economic class or imply agreement.

## A finite time control

Every number below carries a Designed evidence label. This control uses one
ordinary household with four residents and two counted workforce members in one
28-day period. The control does not estimate leisure, domestic work or subsistence
in the United States.

The modeled time endowment is 224 hours per counted workforce member: 448 hours
for this household. This is a declared interval for the modeled commitments,
not every hour lived by all four residents. Children and other residents do not
silently become workers or time contributors.

The example reserves 32 protected
household hours and 64 routine unpaid provisioning hours. Each unmet
person-period food parcel adds a 20-hour provisioning burden. Play must qualify
these policy candidates. The coefficients are not empirical values.

The food unit is the national policy's **person-period food parcel**, weighing
14 kg and opening at 280 currency units. The parcel is not one kilogram or one meal.
No provisioning hour itself produces a food parcel.

Two members attend 160 hours each, including their paid idle hours. The required
food quantity is four parcels. With two available parcels, two remain unmet.

Protected commitments consume 32 hours after attendance. Routine provisioning
costs 64 hours. The unmet-food burden costs another 40. Only 96 hours remain for
these 104 unpaid commitments, leaving eight hours unresolved and no time for
voluntary contribution.

| Account, hours | Unmet food | Two more parcels consumed | Support still in transit |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Modeled endowment | 448 | 448 | 448 |
| Actual attended time, including idle | 320 | 320 | 320 |
| Protected commitments allocated | 32 | 32 | 32 |
| Unpaid commitments requested | 104 | 64 | 104 |
| Unpaid commitments allocated | 96 | 64 | 96 |
| Unpaid commitments unresolved | 8 | 0 | 8 |
| Voluntary contribution available | 0 | 32 | 0 |

Each column reconciles:

`endowment = attended + protected allocated + unpaid allocated + contribution + remaining`

Unresolved commitments are claims on time, not extra performed hours. Paying
last period's overdue wages changes cash, not this period's attendance.
A ledger must not charge 320 hours again as production, handling, maintenance
and idle: those purposes already partition the same attendance.

Actual consumption of two more parcels removes the 40-hour extra burden.
Consumption makes 32 existing hours available. Consumption does not add 40 hours
to the endowment. The household can decline all organizational work.

Suppose two
contributor bodies from this household consent to offer 16 and 24 hours.
Their combined allocation is at most 32. They cannot each draw from separate
32-hour allowances. Incomplete work creates no completed practice product.

## The aid and solidarity choice

At the opening food price, two parcels cost 560 currency units before actual
freight and handling. The organization must have that money and fund the real
order. The engine admits a local commitment in the following period. Consumption
can follow within that period. A remote commitment uses the same finite goods and shared
transport, with availability only after arrival and household distribution.
In-transit support leaves the third column unchanged.

Local relief forgoes a real remote shipment, while remote support can
sustain another community's later independently authorized activity. Neither
choice promises agreement, membership or a political response. The preview must
show the dated price, funded quantity, known route, timing uncertainty, own
effort and foregone alternative. A later receipt must distinguish reserved,
dispatched, arrived, distributed and consumed support.

## Implementation and qualification still required

The current organizer uses fixed captured participant allowances. Those are
appropriate closed controls. The richer campaign needs household bindings and
actual time evidence. The engine constructs an accepted command before material
resolution. It must record resource shortage as unsuccessful or incomplete work.

It must preserve the accepted action. The world must advance when a valid
practice lacks resources. Malformed inputs and failed commits must remain atomic
refusals.

Required witnesses include two bodies with one household, paid idle,
unemployment with continuing needs and late wage payment. Further witnesses cover
shortage after consent, duplicate support admission, remote delay and independent
partner refusal.
Severing recipient consumption, finite contribution or agreement must remove
the respective downstream consequence. Actual national play must test
whether the relief-versus-solidarity choice is understandable and enjoyable.
