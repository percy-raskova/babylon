The opening exposed three actual integration faults:

- Distinct firms could not share one county freight access point. Shared access
  now retains separate stock, orders and accounts and the same finite corridor.
- Period-service processes received stock buffers. They now retain zero output
  stock buffers while their service plans remain executable.
- Empty workplaces were rejected by payroll admission, and multiple resident
  members generated duplicate workplace attendance plans. There is now one plan
  per distinct employment/current-labor principal. A memberless principal needs
  both an explicit zero available-hours row and zero planned hours. Desired work
  can remain unmet without creating workers, funded hours, wages or output.
