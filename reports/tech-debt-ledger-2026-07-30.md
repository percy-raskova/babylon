## 1. The verdict

**Better than it looks on the inert code, worse than it looks on the gates.** Twenty-two of the thirty rows are dormant constructs inside a deliberately frozen engine — they cost nothing at runtime and most of them die with the Python port, so they are a *transcription* problem, not a debt problem. The estate is not rotting. But there is one genuinely systemic failure and it is not on that list: **the player-facing gate layer — fog and the Veil of Money — is wired only into `web/game/engine_bridge.py`, which Amendment V declares legacy, and is called by nothing on the path that actually ships.** I verified at `985f5145` that `src/babylon/game/session.py` and every `src/babylon/projection/vault/render_*.py` contain zero references to `apply_fog`, `apply_political_gates`, `compute_veil_status`, or `gate_value_axis_fields`. That compounds with the second real finding: roughly half the 28 sentinel families are closed hand-curated allowlists whose green summary line reads as "nothing is disconnected" but means "the two-to-five rows I was handed are fine." The gate isn't guarding, and the meta-gate is scoped so it cannot see that. Everything else is cheap or deferrable.
